Executive Committee of the Legislative Council. The bill makes
the following changes to the legislative department cash fund (fund):
! Transfers $12 million from the fund to the general fund;
! Establishes a fund limit, equal to $8 million for the current
state fiscal year, and, thereafter, it adjusts proportionally
with the percentage change in total general fund
appropriations for the legislative branch;
! Limits reversion of unexpended appropriations to the fund
