2 responses to differences, such as mediation, arbitration, facilitation,
3 collaborative decision-making, ombudsperson activities, and restorative
4 justice practices; and
5 WHEREAS, These conflict resolution processes empower
6 individuals, families, communities, organizations, and businesses to foster
7 communication and design solutions that are acceptable and responsive
8 to the needs and interests of all parties involved; and
9 WHEREAS, Conflict resolution is taught and practiced by
10 professionals in many school systems and universities throughout
11 Colorado and the world as a peaceful, nonviolent way of resolving
12 disputes; and
13 WHEREAS, Community-based conflict transformation programs
14 work to strengthen local relationships by fairly and equitably resolving
15 neighborhood and community conflicts and opening dialogues grounded

5 WHEREAS, Alternative dispute resolution (ADR) and restorative

10 increased satisfaction for the parties who are active participants and

14 WHEREAS, The Colorado General Assembly's exemplary
15 collaboration across party lines to enact sound conflict resolution policy

21 Be It Resolved by the Senate of the Seventy-fifth General Assembly

23 (1) That we, the members of the Colorado Senate, join the
24 Governor in recognizing October as Conflict Resolution Month in
25 Colorado;
26 (2) That we invite Coloradans to create events that celebrate and
27 promote the use of productive conflict transformation and dispute
28 resolution in their communities;
29 (3) That we call upon leaders across the state to use Conflict
30 Resolution Month in Colorado as an occasion to discuss the principles
31 and use the practices of conflict resolution in their homes, schools,
32 neighborhoods, congregations, and workplaces;
