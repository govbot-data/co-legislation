1 WHEREAS, FTD is often misdiagnosed as either a psychiatric
2 disorder or a catch-all neurodegenerative disease due to the vast range
3 and diverse combinations of cognitive and behavioral symptoms, and
4 most cases of FTD are diagnosed in people aged 45 to 65--an age group
5 much younger than all other common forms of dementia that typically
6 affect those over 65; and
7 WHEREAS, FTD often negatively changes a person's ability to
8 express emotions and show affection and empathy for loved ones; and
9 WHEREAS, In the behavioral variant of FTD, a person's sense of
10 social etiquette and appropriate behavior can be completely lost; it can
11 also result in a distortion of personality so profound that loved ones may
12 feel they are living with a complete stranger; and
13 WHEREAS, In the language variants of FTD, in addition to
14 Primary Progressive Aphasia (PPA), variants include Semantic Variant
15 PPA (svPPA, also called Semantic Dementia), Nonfluent/Agrammatic
16 Variant PPA (nfvPPA), Logopenic Variant PPA (lvPPA), Behavioral
17 Variant FTD (bvFTD), and FTD with Motor Neuron Disease
18 (FTD-ALS/MND)--noting the 3 PPA subtypes are distinct, but all are
19 considered part of the "language-led" (aphasic) spectrum of FTD; and
20 WHEREAS, Additionally in the language variants of FTD, a
21 person may experience difficulty speaking and understanding grammar,
22 forget the meaning of words, and demonstrate a drastically diminished
23 vocabulary--becoming increasingly hesitant to speak and potentially
24 losing the ability to speak altogether; and
25 WHEREAS, In the movement variants of FTD, a person may
26 experience muscle weakness, falling, loss of balance, difficulty making
27 speech, difficulty swallowing, or choking; and
28 WHEREAS, A formal global epidemiology study of FTD is yet to
29 take place. However, the current estimation is believed to be over 60,000
30 people affected in the United States, which is likely a paltry fraction of
31 the total figure, if accounting for the gap of cases that comprise the initial
32 4 years of misdiagnosis and underdiagnosis in aggregate; and
33 WHEREAS, The Association for Frontotemporal Degeneration is
34 the leading national organization exclusively focused on the spectrum of
35 FTD disorders with a mission to improve the quality of life of people
