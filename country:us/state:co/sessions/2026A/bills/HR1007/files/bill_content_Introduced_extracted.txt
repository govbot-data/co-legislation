7 Be It Resolved by the House of Representatives of the Seventy-fifth
8 General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
9 (1) That we honor the generations of Coloradans whose courage,
10 industry, faith, and devotion to self-government transformed a rugged
11 frontier into a thriving state and whose example continues to inspire the
12 preservation of freedom for future generations;
13 (2) That, as symbolized by our famous, towering,
14 fourteen-thousand-foot peaks, the people of Colorado are encouraged to
15 continue striving toward the highest ideals of freedom, responsibility, and
16 civic virtue;
17 (3) That we call upon all citizens of Colorado to commemorate
18 this historic milestone with gratitude for the blessings of liberty, humility
19 before Providence, and renewed dedication to the principles that have
20 guided the state since its founding in 1876; and
21 (4) That we commit to preserving Colorado for future generations
22 by, among other things, promoting Colorado history and civic education,
23 strengthening infrastructure to protect against future threats or
24 emergencies, and encouraging the making of another one hundred fifty
25 years of colorful Colorado history.
