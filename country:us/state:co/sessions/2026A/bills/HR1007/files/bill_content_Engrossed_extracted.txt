1 WHEREAS, Xenial hospitality-extraordinary kindness extended
2 to explorers, expats, and excited newcomers-expresses the generous
3 character of Coloradans; and
4 WHEREAS, Year after year, youthful yearning and yielding
5 innovation yoke generations together while affirming the importance of
6 preparedness in uncertain times; and
7 WHEREAS, Zealous defense of fundamental freedoms, along with
8 foundational principles rooted in faith, freedom, and enduring moral
9 tradition, underscores Colorado's commitment to liberty as it celebrates
10 one hundred fifty years of statehood; now, therefore,
11 Be It Resolved by the House of Representatives of the Seventy-fifth
12 General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
13 (1) That we honor the generations of Coloradans whose courage,
14 industry, faith, and devotion to self-government transformed a rugged
15 frontier into a thriving state and whose example continues to inspire the
16 preservation of freedom for future generations;
17 (2) That, as symbolized by our famous, towering,
18 fourteen-thousand-foot peaks, the people of Colorado are encouraged to
19 continue striving toward the highest ideals of freedom, responsibility, and
20 civic virtue;
21 (3) That we call upon all citizens of Colorado to commemorate
22 this historic milestone with gratitude for the blessings of liberty, humility
23 before Providence, and renewed dedication to the principles that have
24 guided the state since its founding in 1876; and
25 (4) That we commit to preserving Colorado for future generations
26 by, among other things, protecting our natural resources, including
27 pollinators, promoting Colorado history and civic education,
28 strengthening infrastructure to protect against future threats or
29 emergencies, and encouraging the making of another one hundred fifty
30 years of colorful Colorado history.
