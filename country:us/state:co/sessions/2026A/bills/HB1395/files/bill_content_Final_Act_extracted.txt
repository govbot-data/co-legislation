HOUSE BILL 26-1395
BY REPRESENTATIVE(S) Sirota and Taggart, Brown, Slaugh, Weinberg;
also SENATOR(S) Amabile and Bridges, Kirkmeyer.
CONCERNING THE REPEAL OF THE WILDFIRE RESILIENT HOMES GRANT
PROGRAM ADMINISTERED BY THE DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC SAFETY,
AND, IN CONNECTION THEREWITH, TRANSFERRING THE BALANCE OF
THE WILDFIRE RESILIENT HOMES GRANT PROGRAM CASH FUND TO THE
GENERAL FUND.
Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
SECTION 1. In Colorado Revised Statutes, 24-33.5-1239, add
(4)(c) and (7) as follows:
24-33.5-1239. Wildfire resilient homes grant program - fund -
rules - report - definitions - repeal.
(4) (c) ON JUNE 30, 2026, THE STATE TREASURER SHALL TRANSFER
THE UNEXPENDED AND UNENCUMBERED BALANCE OF THE WILDFIRE
RESILIENT HOMES GRANT PROGRAM CASH FUND TO THE GENERAL FUND.
(7) THIS SECTION IS REPEALED, EFFECTIVE JULY 1, 2026.
