CONCERNING DESIGNATING MARCH AS "ARTS EDUCATION
MONTH" IN COLORADO.
WHEREAS, Arts education, which comprises a rich array of
disciplines such as dance, media arts, music, film and theatre arts, visual
arts, and more, is a well-rounded subject and is an essential element of a
complete education for all students; and
WHEREAS, A substantive and sequential visual and performing arts
curriculum enables students to develop critical thinking and
problem-solving skills, imagination and creativity, discipline and
collaboration, alternative ways to communicate and express feelings and
ideas, and cross-cultural understanding, which support academic success
across the curriculum; and .
WHEREAS, Arts education contributes to increased attendance and
graduation rates; elevates academic achievement; enhances social and
emotional learning; and paves the way for students to achieve college,
career, and citizenship readiness; and
WHEREAS, To succeed in today's economy, students must
masterfully develop skills that business leaders demand in a 21st century
workforce, including the ability to be creative, communicate effectively,
collaborate with peers, think critically, and innovate; and

WHEREAS, Although arts education is part of a well-rounded
education according to federal statute, access to quality arts education is
not widely available or consistent across Colorado; and
WHEREAS, Standards for dance; drama, film, and theatre arts; music;
and visual arts have been fully embedded in the Color~do academic
standards and are updated every six years, but there is no statewide
instructional requirement for elementary schools, middle schools, or high
schools; and
WHEREAS, Colorado is one of only two states. that does not collect
data on course-level offerings in the arts across all districts or data on
student participation in specific forms of art, such as dance, music,
theatre, or visual arts, which leads to difficulty in evaluating the equitable
access to arts education for Colorado students, schools, and districts; and
WHEREAS, The arts are a valued and integral part of life in the
United States and in Colorado, are essential for a complete education, and
contribute immensely to the vibrancy and vitality of communities and the
nation; now, therefore,
Belt Resolved by the Senate oft he Seventy-fifth General Assembly of
the State of Colorado:
(1) That, to align with Music in Our Schools Month, Theatre in Our
Schools Month, Dance in Your Schools Month, and Youth Art Month, all
of which are celebrated nationally in March each year, the Colorado
General Assembly designates March as "Arts Education Month" to
commemorate, celebrate, and advance the vital role that arts learning
plays in a complete and comprehensive education; and
(2) That the state of Colorado recognizes the need to expand equitable
access to quality arts education across the state to serve all students.
