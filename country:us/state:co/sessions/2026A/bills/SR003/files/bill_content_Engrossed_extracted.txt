4 WHEREAS, Although arts education is part of a well-rounded
5 education according to federal statute, access to quality arts education is
6 not widely available or consistent across Colorado; and
7 WHEREAS, Standards for dance; drama, film, and theatre arts;
8 music; and visual arts have been fully embedded in the Colorado
9 academic standards and are updated every six years, but there is no
10 statewide instructional requirement for elementary schools, middle

12 WHEREAS, Colorado is one of only two states that does not
13 collect data on course-level offerings in the arts across all districts or data
14 on student participation in specific forms of art, such as dance, music,
15 theatre, or visual arts, which leads to difficulty in evaluating the equitable
16 access to arts education for Colorado students, schools, and districts; and
17 WHEREAS, The arts are a valued and integral part of life in the

20 nation; now, therefore,
21 Be It Resolved by the Senate of the Seventy-fifth General Assembly
22 of the State of Colorado:
23 (1) That, to align with Music in Our Schools Month, Theatre in
24 Our Schools Month, Dance in Your Schools Month, and Youth Art
25 Month, all of which are celebrated nationally in March each year, the
26 Colorado General Assembly designates March as "Arts Education Month"
27 to commemorate, celebrate, and advance the vital role that arts learning
28 plays in a complete and comprehensive education; and
29 (2) That the state of Colorado recognizes the need to expand
30 equitable access to quality arts education across the state to serve all
31 students.
32 Be It Further Resolved, That copies of this Resolution be sent to
