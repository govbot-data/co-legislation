1 WHEREAS, The Taxpayer's Bill of Rights (TABOR), adopted by
2 Colorado voters in 1992 as section 20 of article X of the Colorado
3 Constitution, was enacted to ensure that taxpayers are directly involved
4 in major fiscal decisions affecting the collection and expenditure of
5 public funds at the state and local level; and
6 WHEREAS, Prior to the adoption of the Taxpayer's Bill of Rights,
7 taxpayers were often excluded from decisions involving tax increases,
8 debt obligations, and the growth of government spending, prompting
9 Colorado voters to establish constitutional protections requiring taxpayer
10 consent and transparency in fiscal policy; and
11 WHEREAS, The Taxpayer's Bill of Rights affirms that the consent
12 of the governed is required before government may impose new taxes,
13 increase tax rates, extend expiring taxes, increase mill levies, incur debt,
14 or enact tax policy changes that directly cause a net increase in tax
