CONCERNING DRY NEEDLING BY OCCUPATIONAL THERAPISTS.
Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
SECTION 1. In Colorado Revised Statutes, add 12-270-109.5 as
follows:
12-270-109.5. Dry needling - rules.
(1) ON AND AFTER SEPTEMBER 1, 2027, AN OCCUPATIONAL
THERAPIST MAY PERFORM DRY NEEDLING IF THE OCCUPATIONAL THERAPIST:
(a) HAS THE KNOWLEDGE, SKILL, ABILITY, AND DOCUMENTED
COMPETENCY TO PERFORM THE ACT;
(b) HAS SUCCESSFULLY COMPLETED A DRY NEEDLING COURSE OF
STUDY THAT MEETS THE SUPERVISORIAL, EDUCATIONAL, AND CLINICAL
________

PREREQUISITES ESTABLISHED BY RULE PURSUANT TO SUBSECTION (2) OF THIS
SECTION; AND
(c) OBTAINS A WRITTEN INFORMED CONSENT FROM EACH PATIENT
FOR DRY NEEDLING, INCLUDING:
(I) INFORMATION CONCERNING THE POTENTIAL BENEFITS AND RISKS
OF DRY NEEDLING; AND
(II) A STATEMENT THAT THE OCCUPATIONAL THERAPIST PERFORMING
DRY NEEDLING IS NOT AN ACUPUNCTURIST.
(2) ON OR BEFORE JUNE 1, 2027, THE DIRECTOR SHALL ADOPT RULES
TO ESTABLISH THE REQUIREMENTS FOR AN OCCUPATIONAL THERAPIST TO
PERFORM DRY NEEDLING IN ORDER TO ENSURE ADEQUATE PROTECTION OF
THE PUBLIC. AT A MINIMUM, THE RULES MUST ESTABLISH REQUIREMENTS
THAT ARE EQUIVALENT TO THE REQUIREMENTS ESTABLISHED IN RULES
ADOPTED BY THE STATE PHYSICAL THERAPY BOARD PURSUANT TO SECTION
12-285-116 (5)(b) AUTHORIZING A PHYSICAL THERAPIST TO PERFORM DRY
NEEDLING. PRIOR TO ADOPTING THE RULES, THE DIRECTOR SHALL SEEK INPUT
FROM THE COLORADO MEDICAL BOARD CREATED IN SECTION 12-240-105 (1).
(3) NOTWITHSTANDING SECTION 12-200-103 (5), THE PERFORMANCE
OF DRY NEEDLING BY AN OCCUPATIONAL THERAPIST IN ACCORDANCE WITH
THIS SECTION IS NOT THE PRACTICE OF ACUPUNCTURE AND IS NOT A
VIOLATION OF SECTION 12-200-108.
SECTION 2. Act subject to petition - effective date. This act
takes effect at 12:01 a.m. on the day following the expiration of the
ninety-day period after final adjournment of the general assembly (August
12, 2026, if adjournment sine die is on May 13, 2026); except that, if a
referendum petition is filed pursuant to section 1 (3) of article V of the state
constitution against this act or an item, section, or part of this act within
such period, then the act, item, section, or part will not take effect unless
