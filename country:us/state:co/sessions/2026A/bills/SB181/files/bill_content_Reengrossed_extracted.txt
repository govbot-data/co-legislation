Joint Budget Committee. Where current law requires, the bill
now permits counties to offer households that demonstrate good cause an
extension of Colorado works program (works program) assistance beyond
the 60-month lifetime maximum.
The bill suspends the works program basic cash assistance grant
cost of living adjustment during the 2026-27 and 2027-28 state fiscal
years.
