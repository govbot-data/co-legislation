CONCERNING THE DESIGNATION OF FEBRUARY 20, 2026,
AS COLORADO FFA DAY.
WHEREAS, The Colorado FFA is dedicated to making a positive
difference in the lives of students by developing their potential for
premier leadership, personal growth, and career success through
agricultural education; and
WHEREAS, Founded in 1928, the Future Farmers of America has
brought together students, teachers, and agribusinesses to solidify support
for agricultural education; and
WHEREAS, In 1988, the official name was changed to the National
FFA Organization; this change was made to recognize that FFA is not
only for those interested in farming but also for those with more diverse
interests in the agriculture industry, encompassing science, business, and
technology, within and to support production farming; and
WHEREAS, The Colorado FFA Foundation seeks human, financial,

and in-kind resources to support agricultural education in Colorado for
secondary and postsecondary students, adults participating in agricultural
education, teachers of agricultural education, and FFA alumni; and
WHEREAS, There are opportunities within the Colorado FFA
Association for all members in grades seven through twelve and in
college to be involved in leadership and agriculture career development;
and
WHEREAS, Whether a member grew up on a farm, in a suburb, or in
an urban area, if they have a passion for judging and public speaking,
want to explore the depths of agriscience, or get hands-on experience
working in the industry, they are able to acquire such experiences through
the diverse opportunities of agricultural education and the FFA; and
WHEREAS, We recognize that the Colorado FFA Association and
Foundation are growing leaders, building communities, and strengthening
agriculture through youth development; now, therefore,
Be It Resolved by the Senate of the Seventy-fifth General Assembly of
the State of Colorado, the House of Representatives concurring herein:
That we, the members of the General Assembly, proudly recognize
and designate "Colorado FFA Day" on February 20, 2026, in celebration
of the many students who have gone and continue to go through the FFA
program.
Be It Further Resolved, That copies of this Joint Resolution be sent to
