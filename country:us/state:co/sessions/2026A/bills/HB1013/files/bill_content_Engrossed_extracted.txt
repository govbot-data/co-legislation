(Note: This summary applies to this bill as introduced and does
not reflect any amendments that may be subsequently adopted. If this bill

The bill authorizes landlords to use a ratio utility billing system to
allocate utility charges for a residential premises to individual tenants.
The landlord may charge tenants a utility bill using a ratio utility billing
system if the landlord meets certain requirements, such as:
