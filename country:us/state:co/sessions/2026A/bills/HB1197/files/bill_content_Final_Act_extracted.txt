(27)(a)(IV); and add (37)(a)(III) as follows:

functions for repeal, continuation, or reestablishment - legislative
declaration - repeal.
(27) (a) The following agencies, functions, or both, are scheduled
for repeal on September 1, 2026:
(IV) The registration and regulation of vessels by the department of
natural resources in accordance with article 13 of title 33, C.R.S.;

SECTION 2. In Colorado Revised Statutes, amend 33-13-116 as
follows:
33-13-116. Repeal of sections.
Sections 33-13-103, 33-13-104, 33-13-105, and 33-13-107 and this
section are repealed, effective September 1, 2026 SEPTEMBER 1, 2036. Prior
to the repeal, the function of registration and regulation of vessels shall be
reviewed as provided for in section 24-34-104.
SECTION 3. In Colorado Revised Statutes, 33-13-102, amend (5);
and add (5.3) as follows:
33-13-102. Definitions.
As used in this article 13, unless the context otherwise requires:
(5) (a) "Vessel" means every description of watercraft TYPE OF
WATERCRAFT, EXCEPT A SEAPLANE ON THE WATER, used or capable of being
used as a means of transportation of persons and property on the water,
including all types of stand-up paddleboards BUT DOES NOT INCLUDE
FISHING AIDS, AIR MATTRESSES, BEACH AND WATER TOYS, OR INNER TUBES.
(b) "Vessel" does not include:
(I) Any single-chambered, air-inflated devices that are not stand-up
paddleboards; or
(II) Seaplanes.
(5.3) "VESSEL LIVERY" MEANS A PERSON THAT ADVERTISES AND
OFFERS A VESSEL FOR RENT OR LEASE TO ANOTHER PERSON IN EXCHANGE
FOR ANY KIND OF PAYMENT.

SECTION 4. In Colorado Revised Statutes, amend 33-13-107 as
follows:
33-13-107. Vessel liveries - repeal.
(1) The owner or operator of A vessel livery shall keep a record of
the name and address of each person who THAT hires any vessel that is
designed or permitted to be operated as a vessel, the hull identification
number of the vessel, and the departure date and time and the expected date
and time of return of the vessel. Such records shall be preserved for at least
thirty days THREE YEARS after the vessel is to be returned and shall be ARE
subject to inspection by the division. Any person who THAT violates this
subsection (1) commits a civil infraction and, upon conviction, shall be
punished by a fine of one hundred dollars.
(2) Neither the owner nor operator of A vessel livery nor such
owner's or operator's OR THE VESSEL LIVERY'S agent or employee shall NOT
permit any vessel to depart from his or her THE VESSEL LIVERY'S premises
unless such vessel is equipped and registered as required by this article 13
and rules promulgated ADOPTED pursuant to this article 13. Any person who
THAT violates this subsection (2) commits a civil infraction and, upon
conviction, shall be punished by a fine of one hundred dollars.

SECTION 7. Act subject to petition - effective date applicability.
(1) This act takes effect at 12:01 a.m. on the day following the expiration
of the ninety-day period after final adjournment of the general assembly
(August 12, 2026, if adjournment sine die is on May 13, 2026); except that,
if a referendum petition is filed pursuant to section 1 (3) of article V of the
state constitution against this act or an item, section, or part of this act
within such period, then the act, item, section, or part will not take effect
unless approved by the people at the general election to be held in
November 2026 and, in such case, will take effect on the date of the official
declaration of the vote thereon by the governor.
