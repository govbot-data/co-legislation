2 funded schools, laid railroads, and drew people from around the world
3 seeking opportunity; and
4 WHEREAS, Mining contributed immensely to making the territory
5 into a state, helped the rugged landscape evolve into a thriving economy,
6 and responded when called upon to fulfill a critical role in the production
7 of minerals in support of the Nation during times of war; and
8 WHEREAS, The mining industry and the Colorado Mining
9 Association prioritize continual improvements to safety performance and
10 mitigation of environmental impacts as mining practices advance and
11 innovate; and
12 WHEREAS, The mining industry celebrates its workforce and the
13 communities in which it operates across Colorado representing a
14 partnership that emphasizes safety, transparency, and ongoing
15 engagement; and
16 WHEREAS, Colorado Mining Association members have
17 significantly contributed to the reclamation of legacy mine sites, with
18 substantial resources dedicated to reclaiming legacy mine lands; and
19 WHEREAS, The Colorado Mining Association maintains a strong
20 working relationship with the State of Colorado and its regulatory
21 agencies, and, for over 25 years, the Colorado Mining Association has
22 partnered with the Colorado Division of Reclamation, Mining, and Safety
23 in the Department of Natural Resources to promote and recognize
24 excellence in environmental stewardship and mine safety; and
25 WHEREAS, The Colorado Mining Association has maintained a
26 Memorandum of Understanding with the U.S. Bureau of Land
27 Management since 1976 to foster open communication and collaboration
28 for the responsible production of mineral resources in Colorado; and
29 WHEREAS, Thanks to these long-standing collaborative
30 relationships, Colorado's mining industry has earned a global reputation
31 for exceeding some of the world's most rigorous environmental and safety
32 standards; and
