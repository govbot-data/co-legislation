6 the territorial economy, population growth, and path to Colorado's
7 statehood; and

9 role in the history of the State and represented its earliest employers and
10 mining communities; and

1 WHEREAS, Colorado has among the richest mineral endowments
2 in the Nation, and is the global leader in molybdenum production and a
3 significant producer of gold, coal, uranium, vanadium, and industrial
4 minerals and has the potential for additional copper, silver, and other
5 critical minerals development in the future; and
6 WHEREAS, The mining industry in Colorado contributes
7 approximately $7 billion to Colorado's GDP each year, has among the
8 highest industry sector pay rates in the state with an average annual salary
9 of $112,000, and supports nearly 57,000 direct and indirect jobs, making
10 Colorado the hub of the professional mining sector in the United States;
11 and
12 WHEREAS, The mining industry in Colorado produces minerals
13 that support other important Colorado industries, including energy
14 production and transmission, aerospace and technology, medical
15 equipment, infrastructure, and many others, contributing to the nation's
16 energy, national security, and advanced technology objectives; and
17 WHEREAS, The Colorado Mining Association is well positioned
18 to remain an important part of Colorado's economy, supporting jobs,
19 economic development, and the production of minerals needed for the
20 state's industries and future growth; now, therefore,
21 Be It Resolved by the Senate of the Seventy-fifth General Assembly
22 of the State of Colorado, the House of Representatives concurring herein:
23 That the General Assembly recognizes the Colorado Mining
24 Association for its foundational role in Colorado's history and its
25 continued representation of the mining industry to support responsible
