1 WHEREAS, Women formally became permanent members of the
2 United States Armed Forces with the passage of the Women's Armed
3 Services Integration Act of 1948, paving the way for expanded
4 leadership, responsibility, and recognition within the military; and

7 Air Force, Marine Corps, Space Force, Coast Guard, Reserves, and
