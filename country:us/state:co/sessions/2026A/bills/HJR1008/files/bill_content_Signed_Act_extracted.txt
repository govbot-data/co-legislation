CONCERNING THE RECOGNITION AND HONORING OF
BRAV E WOMEN VETERANS AND WOMEN SERVING IN THE
UNITED STATES ARMED FORCES.
WHEREAS, Women have courageously served the United States in
military roles since the founding of our nation, dating back to the
American Revolutionary War in 177 5 when women served as nurses,
spies, and couriers and in defense roles alongside the Continental Anny;
and
WHEREAS, Throughout every major national and global conflict,
including the Civil War, World Warl, World WarlI, the Korean War, the
Vietnam War, the Gulf War, the Global War on Terror, and ongoing
missions around the world, women have answered the call to serve with
honor, sacrifice, and unwavering patriotism; and

WHEREAS, Women formally became permanent members of the
United States Armed Forces with the passage of the Women's Armed
Services Integration Act of 1948, paving the way for expanded
leadership, responsibility, and recognition within the military; and
WHEREAS, Women have served with distinction across all branches
of the United States Armed Forces, including the Army, Navy, Air Force,
Marine Corps, Space Force, Coast Guard, Reserves, and National Guard,
contributing critical skills, leadership, innovation, operational excellence,
and a warrior spirit; and
WHEREAS, Women have served the United States Armed Forces in
many specialities, including as pilots, sailors, infantry leaders, intelligence
specialists, doctors, medics, engineers, cyber operators, scientists, and
builders, and, by 2016, all military positions were open to women,
reflecting their proven capability, resilience, and commitment to mission
success; and
WHEREAS, The establishment of the United States Space Force in
2019 further expanded opportunities for women to lead and protect our
nation in emerging and highly technical domains critical to national
security; and
WHEREAS, Women veterans and service members have endured
extraordinary sacrifices, including time away from family, physical and
emotional hardship, and the lasting impacts of service, all while
strengthening the readiness, effectiveness, and moral foundation of the
United States Armed Forces; and
WHEREAS, The contributions of women in uniform have not only
defended freedom and democracy at home and abroad but have also
inspired future generations ofw omen to serve their country with strength,
confidence, courage, and honor; now, therefore,
