SENATE JOINT RESOLUTION 26-024
101 CONCERNING THE DESIGNATION OF MAY 2026 AS "MOTORCYCLE
102 SAFETY AWARENESS MONTH".
1 WHEREAS, The month of May marks the traditional start of the
2 motorcycle riding season; and
3 WHEREAS, Motorcycles occupy a significant place in Colorado
4 and American history, having made contributions to numerous military
5 campaigns, law enforcement operations, and the culture of the American
6 West; and
7 WHEREAS, Over 8 million motorcycles are registered in the
8 United States, with over 200,000 registered in Colorado, and they are
9 used on public roads and highways, providing reliable day-to-day
10 transportation and recreational opportunities; and

1 WHEREAS, Because of their light weight, fuel efficiency, and low
2 impact on our highways, motorcycles should be encouraged as a viable
3 transportation alternative; and
4 WHEREAS, Motorcycles take up less space on our highways and
5 parking areas, thus reducing traffic congestion; and
6 WHEREAS, Motorcyclists are provided legal access to federally
7 funded High Occupancy Vehicle lanes to increase traffic flow while
8 giving motorcyclists a safer environment for travel; and
9 WHEREAS, Riders and passengers of motorcycles face countless
10 hazards and unforeseen circumstances on the road; and
11 WHEREAS, 147 motorcyclists were killed on Colorado roadways
12 last year; and
13 WHEREAS, State and national "Share the Road" campaigns have
14 been created to remind all motorists to be extra alert in order to increase
15 motorcyclists' safety; and
16 WHEREAS, Studies show that many motorcyclists involved in
17 accidents did not have formal rider training; and
18 WHEREAS, Many causes of motorcycle crashes may be attributed
19 to lack of experience or failure to appreciate the unique operating
20 characteristics and limitations of motorcycles, as well as the failure of
21 other motorists to share the road with motorcyclists; and
22 WHEREAS, The motorcycling community has made an ongoing
23 commitment to increasing awareness of appropriate safety measures and
24 increased participation in rider training programs; and
25 WHEREAS, Many rider training programs are offered throughout
26 Colorado, both for beginner and experienced riders, to prepare them to
27 operate a motorcycle in the safest possible manner; and
28 WHEREAS, Rider education, training, and motorcycle awareness
29 are important steps in motorcycle accident prevention; now, therefore,
30 Be It Resolved by the Senate of the Seventy-fifth General Assembly
-2- SJR26-024

1 of the State of Colorado, the House of Representatives concurring herein:
2
3 That we, the members of the Colorado General Assembly,
4 recognize and designate May 2026 as "Motorcycle Safety Awareness
5 Month" to promote safety for all motorcycle riders through education and
6 training and to encourage all motorists to share the road in Colorado.
