Bill Summary
(Note: This summary applies to this bill as introduced and does
not reflect any amendments that may be subsequently adopted. If this bill
passes third reading in the house of introduction, a bill summary that
applies to the reengrossed version of this bill will be available at
http://leg.colorado.gov.)
The bill requires a minor to have written permission of the minor's
parent or legal guardian to obtain an instruction permit to drive a
motorcycle. The bill does not apply to foster children or emancipated
minors.

1 Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
2 SECTION 1. Short title. The short title of this act is the "Cole
3 Bradley Act".
4 SECTION 2. Legislative declaration. (1) The general assembly
5 finds and declares that:
6 (a) Motorcycles inherently provide significantly less occupant
7 protection than passenger motor vehicles, leaving operators and
8 passengers fully exposed to impact forces in the event of a crash, loss of
9 control, or roadway hazard;
10 (b) In 2024, the state of Colorado recorded 165 motorcycle
11 fatalities, which is the highest number of motorcycle deaths in state
12 history and represents a 57% increase since 2015;
13 (c) In 2024, motorcycle fatalities accounted for approximately
14 24% of all traffic fatalities in Colorado, despite motorcycles comprising
15 only approximately 3% of registered vehicles statewide, demonstrating
16 a disproportionate risk of death associated with motorcycle operation;
17 (d) State crash data for 2024 indicate that approximately 80% of
18 motorcycle crashes resulted in injury, with injuries frequently involving
19 traumatic brain injury, spinal trauma, or permanent disability;
20 (e) Minors are particularly vulnerable to motorcycle-related
21 injuries and fatalities due to ongoing physical, neurological, and cognitive
22 development, including limitations in hazard recognition, risk assessment,
23 judgment, and reaction time;
24 (f) Colorado law currently recognizes the heightened vulnerability
25 of minors by requiring helmet use for motorcycle operators and
26 passengers under 18 years old; however, protective equipment alone does
-2- 1079

1 not sufficiently mitigate the inherent risks of motorcycle operation by
2 minors;
3 (g) A substantial number of motorcycle fatalities in Colorado
4 involve riders not wearing helmets, and, even when helmets are worn,
5 minors remain at elevated risk due to the absence of structural protections
6 on motorcycles;
7 (h) Colorado's high-speed roadways, mountainous terrain, variable
8 weather conditions, and increasing traffic congestion further increase the
9 dangers associated with motorcycle operation, particularly for young and
10 inexperienced riders;
11 (i) Permitting minors to obtain motorcycle endorsements or
12 operate motorcycles based solely on consent from any individual over 21
13 years old does not adequately ensure responsible oversight, informed
14 decision-making, or appropriate evaluation of a minor's readiness to
15 safely operate a motorcycle;
16 (j) Requiring parental or legal guardian consent:
17 (I) Provides a higher level of accountability;
18 (II) Aligns motorcycle endorsement requirements with Colorado's
19 existing youth safety frameworks; and
20 (III) Helps ensure that minors receive appropriate training,
21 supervision, and assessment prior to operating motorcycles on public
22 roadways;
23 (k) Motorcycle-related injuries and fatalities involving minors
24 impose significant emotional and financial burdens on families and
25 substantial costs to the state through emergency medical response,
26 long-term medical care, rehabilitation, and disability support services; and
27 (l) The state of Colorado has a compelling interest in protecting
-3- 1079

1 the health, safety, and welfare of minors and, in high-risk activities, has
2 long exercised its authority to impose age-based safety requirements,
3 including graduated driver licensing systems and child passenger safety
4 laws.
5 (2) Therefore, the general assembly declares that it is necessary
6 and appropriate to enact legislation regulating motorcycle operation and
7 endorsement eligibility involving minors, including consent and training
8 requirements, in order to protect Colorado's youth.
