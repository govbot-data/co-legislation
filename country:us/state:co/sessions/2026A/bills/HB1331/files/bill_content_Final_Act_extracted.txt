CONCERNING MODIFICATIONS TO LEGISLATIVE INTERIM ACTIVITIES, AND, IN
CONNECTION THEREWITH, REDUCING AN APPROPRIATION.
Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
SECTION 1. In Colorado Revised Statutes, 2-3-303.3, add (3)(g),
(3)(h), and (4) as follows:
2-3-303.3. Interim committees - repeal.
(3) (g) (I) NOTWITHSTANDING ANY PROVISION OF THIS SECTION, THE
LEGISLATIVE COUNCIL SHALL NOT PRIORITIZE ANY INTERIM COMMITTEES OR
APPROVE ANY TASK FORCES FOR THE 2026 INTERIM. NOTHING IN THIS
SUBSECTION (3)(g) PROHIBITS THE EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE OF THE
LEGISLATIVE COUNCIL, WHETHER BEFORE OR AFTER THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY
HAS ADJOURNED, FROM APPROVING AN INTERIM COMMITTEE BY RESOLUTION
FOR THE 2026 INTERIM IN THE MANNER SPECIFIED IN SUBSECTION (3)(c) OF
THIS SECTION.

SECTION 19. Appropriation - adjustments to 2026 legislative
appropriation bill. (1) Except as provided in subsection (2) of this section,
to implement this act, appropriations made in House Bill 26-1333 for the
2026-27 state fiscal year for the legislative department are adjusted as
follows:
(a) The general fund appropriation for the general assembly is
decreased by $183,699;
(b) The general fund appropriation for the legislative council is
decreased by $161,162, and the related FTE is decreased by 1.9 FTE; and
(c) The general fund appropriation for the committee on legal
services is decreased by $108,336, and the related FTE is decreased by 1.4
FTE.
(2) Subsection (1) of this section does not require a reduction of an
appropriation in House Bill 26-1333 for the 2026-27 state fiscal year if:

(a) The amount of the general fund appropriation made in House
Bill 26-1333 for the 2026-27 state fiscal year for the general assembly is
less than the amount of the adjustments required in subsection (1)(a) of this
section;
(b) The amount of the general fund appropriation made in House
Bill 26-1333 for the 2026-27 state fiscal year for the legislative council is
less than the amount of the adjustments required in subsection (1)(b) of this

(c) The amount of the general fund appropriation made in House
Bill 26-1333 for the 2026-27 state fiscal year for the committee on legal

include an appropriation for the legislative department.
SECTION 20. Effective date. This act takes effect upon passage;
except that section 19 of this act takes effect only if House Bill 26-1333 for
the 2026-27 state fiscal year becomes law, in which case section 19 takes
effect upon the effective date of this act or of the House Bill 26-1333 for
state fiscal year 2026-27, whichever is later.
