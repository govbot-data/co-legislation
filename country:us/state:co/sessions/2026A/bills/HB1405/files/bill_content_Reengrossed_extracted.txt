(Note: This summary applies to this bill as introduced and does
not reflect any amendments that may be subsequently adopted. If this bill

Joint Budget Committee. The bill requires the state treasurer to
make certain transfers of money from certain cash funds to the general
fund.
On June 12, 2026, the state treasurer is required to transfer
$16,747 from the local governmental entity backfill cash fund to the
general fund (section 30 of the bill).
