11 Be It Resolved by the House of Representatives of the Seventy-fifth
12 General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
13 That we, the members of the Colorado House of Representatives,
14 reaffirm our commitment to the goals and principles of our
15 responsibilities as stewards of the Earth. In recognition of 2026 Earth Day

17 obligation to limit waste and protect and enhance the long-term health of
18 the state's natural environment, economy, and community, we support
19 Earth Day and International Day of Zero Waste by:
20 (1) Inviting and encouraging all citizens, businesses,
21 organizations, schools, clubs, congregations, neighborhoods, and families
22 to participate in community International Day of Zero Waste events on
23 March 30, 2026, and Earth Day activities on April 22, 2026, and to
24 engage in environmentally sound and waste-reducing practices in their
25 everyday lives;
26 (2) Using the observance of International Day of Zero Waste and
27 Earth Day as a springboard for exploring new avenues for sustainability
28 and waste reduction;
29 (3) Demonstrating leadership and continually evaluating state
30 operations, facilities, and services to ensure the highest standard of
31 efficiency, sustainability, waste reduction, and environmental protection;
32 (4) Forging partnerships with local organizations, environmental
33 professionals, businesses, and citizens to encourage the adoption of
34 statewide sustainability practices and the development of green
35 technologies, and become a model for the region and country; and
