alternative process that is accessible to persons with disabilities. A

The bill clarifies that these duties are duties of the state political
party and that a person who is subjected to a violation of a state political
party's duties regarding these accessibility requirements may file suit
against the state political party. An individual, including a member of or
volunteer for a political party, or a local political party may not be held
liable for a violation of the state political party's duties regarding these
accessibility requirements.
