BY REPRESENTATIVE(S) Brown and Taggart, Sirota;

CONCERNING REDUCING AN APPROPRIATION FOR THE COLORADO ACADEMIC
ACCELERATOR GRANT PROGRAM.
Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
SECTION 1. In Colorado Revised Statutes, 22-2-146.6, amend (9)
as follows:
22-2-146.6. Colorado academic accelerator grant program -
report - rules - definitions - repeal.
(9) (a) For the 2023-24 budget year, the general assembly shall
appropriate twenty-four million five hundred thousand dollars to the
department. The department shall have HAS the authority to spend funds
APPROPRIATED FOR THE 2023-24 BUDGET YEAR continuously through the
2026-27 budget year for the purposes of this section. Any unexpended funds
that remain at the end of the 2026-27 budget year revert back to the general
fund.
