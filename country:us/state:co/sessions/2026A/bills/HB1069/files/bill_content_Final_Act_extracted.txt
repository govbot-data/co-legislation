SECTION 4. In Colorado Revised Statutes, add 25.5-4-436 as
follows:
25.5-4-436. Transportation by ambulance or other vehicle -
treatment on scene of a medical emergency - evaluation by telemedicine
- definitions.
(1) ON AND AFTER JANUARY 1, 2027, THE STATE DEPARTMENT SHALL
REIMBURSE:
(a) AN AMBULANCE SERVICE FOR GROUND TRANSPORTATION BY AN
AMBULANCE OR OTHER APPROPRIATELY SITUATED VEHICLE TO A HOSPITAL
OR OTHER DESTINATION AS DEEMED APPROPRIATE BY THE AMBULANCE
SERVICE'S MEDICAL DIRECTOR;
(b) AN AMBULANCE SERVICE FOR TREATMENT ON THE SCENE OF A
MEDICAL EMERGENCY, WHICH TREATMENT DOES NOT RESULT IN GROUND
TRANSPORTATION; AND
PAGE 4-HOUSE BILL 26-1069

(c) A QUALIFIED PROVIDER, AN AMBULANCE SERVICE, OR AN AGENCY
PROVIDING OUT-OF-HOSPITAL SERVICES FOR EVALUATION BY TELEMEDICINE
OF A PERSON BEING TREATED BY AN AMBULANCE SERVICE OR AN AGENCY
PROVIDING OUT-OF-HOSPITAL SERVICES FOR THE PURPOSE OF PREVENTING
THE NEED TO TRANSPORT THE PERSON TO A HOSPITAL. TELEMEDICINE SHALL
BE BILLED USING AN OFFICE OR OTHER OUTPATIENT SERVICES RATE AND NOT
USING AN EMERGENCY DEPARTMENT RATE. AN AMBULANCE SERVICE OR AN
AGENCY PROVIDING OUT-OF-HOSPITAL SERVICES IS NOT REQUIRED TO
PROCURE TELEMEDICINE FOR A COVERED PERSON AS A CONDITION OF
PAYMENT.
(2) AS USED IN THIS SECTION, UNLESS THE CONTEXT OTHERWISE
REQUIRES:
(a) "AMBULANCE SERVICE" HAS THE MEANING SET FORTH IN SECTION
25-3.5-103 (3).
(b) (I) "OUT-OF-HOSPITAL SERVICES" MEANS FURNISHING ANY
NECESSARY GOODS AND SERVICES OUTSIDE OF A HOSPITAL SETTING FOR THE
PURPOSE OF PREVENTING, ALLEVIATING, CURING, OR HEALING HUMAN
ILLNESS, A PHYSICAL DISABILITY, A PHYSICAL INJURY, OR A SUBSTANCE USE
DISORDER WHILE RESPONDING TO AN EMERGENCY OR OTHER HEALTH-CARE
CONDITION.
(II) "OUT-OF-HOSPITAL SERVICES" DOES NOT INCLUDE PREHOSPITAL
SETTING TRANSPORTS.
