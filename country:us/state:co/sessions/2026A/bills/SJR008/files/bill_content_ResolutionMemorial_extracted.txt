CONCERNING HONORING COLORADO'S 2026 WINTER
OLYMPIANS AND PARALYMPIANS.
WHEREAS, The Olympic and Paralympic Winter Games represent
the highest level of international athletic competition, celebrating
excellence, perseverance, sportsmanship, and the unifying power of sport;
and
WHEREAS, Colorado has long been a national leader in winter
sports, with world-class mountain terrain, training facilities, and a strong
outdoor recreation culture that help develop athletes capable of competing
on the international stage; and
WHEREAS, Athletes from Colorado who qualified for the 2026
Olympic and Paralympic Winter Games demonstrate extraordinary
dedication, discipline, and resilience, often training for years while
balancing education, employment, and commitments to their families and
communities; and

WHEREAS, While wearing the uniform of the United States of
America and competing before a global audience, Colorado's Olympic
and Paralympic athletes proudly represent our nation with honor and
distinction, serving as ambassadors for American values including
perseverance, teamwork, fair competition, and respect for others while
also representing the state of Colorado and their hometowns, many of
them rural and mountain communities; and
WHEREAS, Colorado is home to nationally and internationally
recognized training facilities that support elite athletic development,
including the U.S. Olympic and Paralympic Training Center in Colorado
Springs, the U.S. Ski and Snowboard Team training center at Copper
Mountain in Summit County, and numerous additional venues across the
state that are regularly used by U.S. Olympic and Paralympic teams; and
WHEREAS, The following athletes, each of whom has a meaningful
connection to the state of Colorado, qualified for and are competing in the
2026 Olympic or Paralympic Winter Games:

WHEREAS, The accomplishments of these athletes reflect not only
individual excellence, but also the extraordinary commitment and
sacrifice of their parents and families, as well as the strength of the
communities, training institutions, and public investments that support
athletic development in Colorado and across the United States; now,
therefore,
Be It Resolved by the Senate of the Seventy-fifth General Assembly of
the State of Colorado, the House of Representatives concurring herein:
That we, the members of the Colorado General Assembly:
(1) Hereby honor and commend the Colorado athletes who qualified
for and are competing in the 2026 Olympic and Paralympic Winter
Games, recognizing their exceptional achievements and their service in
representing the United States of America and the state of Colorado with
honor on the world stage; and
(2) Express our sincere appreciation to the parents and families of
these athletes, whose unwavering support, sacrifice, and encouragement
made these achievements possible, as well as to the coaches, trainers,
support staff, communities, and training facilities across Colorado that
helped prepare these athletes to compete at the highest international level.
