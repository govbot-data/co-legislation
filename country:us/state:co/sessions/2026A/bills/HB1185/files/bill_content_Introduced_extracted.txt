Sunset Process - House Judiciary Committee. The department
of regulatory agencies (DORA) conducted a sunset review of the cold
case task force (task force) in the department of public safety
(department) and recommended:
! Authorizing the executive director of the department to
appoint additional task force members;
! Extending the task force until September 1, 2039; and
! Changing the type of sunset review that is performed by
DORA.
The bill implements DORA's recommendations.
