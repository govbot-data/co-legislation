CONCERNING EXPANDING PARTICIPATION BY MEMBERS OF THE AURARIA
BOARD OF DIRECTORS ADVISORY COMMITTEES.
Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
SECTION 1. Short title. The short title of this act is the "Auraria
Student and Faculty Board Voting Rights Act of 2026".
SECTION 2. Legislative declaration - legislative intent.
(1) The general assembly finds and declares that:
(a) Student and faculty representation in postsecondary institutions'
institutional governance promotes accountability, transparency, and
responsiveness to the educational needs of the student body;
(b) The Auraria higher education center has 3 separately governed

institutions whose students and faculty are materially affected by decisions
of the Auraria board of directors, known as the Auraria board;
(c) Granting the elected student and faculty members of the Auraria
board full voting rights strengthens shared governance by ensuring that
directly elected student and faculty representatives participate substantively
in decisions that affect students; and
(d) It is in the public interest to provide the elected student and
faculty members of the Auraria board with voting rights equal to that of
other board members.
(2) The general assembly further declares that this act does not alter
any statutory eligibility requirement for the elected faculty member of the
Auraria board, including residency requirements, except as expressly
provided in the act.
(3) It is the intent of the general assembly that:
(a) The Auraria board and each of the governing boards of the
constituent institutions of the Auraria higher education center will update
their policies, bylaws, and rules, including rules of procedure, as applicable
and as necessary to comply with the amendments to Auraria board
members' authority made in this act; and
(b) A student or faculty member serving on the Auraria board on the
effective date of this act assumes the voting rights granted to that member
in this act without needing to be reelected to the Auraria board.
