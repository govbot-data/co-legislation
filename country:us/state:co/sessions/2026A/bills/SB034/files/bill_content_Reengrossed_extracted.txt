Under current law, the members of the Auraria board of directors
who are the representatives of the student advisory committee and faculty
advisory committee are nonvoting members. The bill makes those
members voting members of the board. Concerning the representative of
the student advisory committee, the bill repeals the requirement that the
student be a full-time student and reduces the Colorado residency

4 SECTION 2. Legislative declaration - legislative intent.
5 (1) The general assembly finds and declares that:
6 (a) Student and faculty representation in postsecondary
7 institutions' institutional governance promotes accountability,
8 transparency, and responsiveness to the educational needs of the student
9 body;
10 (b) The Auraria higher education center has 3 separately governed
11 institutions whose students and faculty are materially affected by
12 decisions of the Auraria board of directors, known as the Auraria board;
13 (c) Granting the elected student and faculty members of the
14 Auraria board full voting rights strengthens shared governance by
15 ensuring that directly elected student and faculty representatives
16 participate substantively in decisions that affect students; and
17 (d) It is in the public interest to provide the elected student and
18 faculty members of the Auraria board with voting rights equal to that of
19 other board members.
20 (2) The general assembly further declares that this act does not
21 alter any statutory eligibility requirement for the elected faculty member
22 of the Auraria board, including residency requirements, except as
23 expressly provided in the act.
