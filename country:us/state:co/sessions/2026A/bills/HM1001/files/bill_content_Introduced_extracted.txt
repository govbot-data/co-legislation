101 MEMORIALIZING FORMER REPRESENTATIVE DAN WILLIAMS.
1 WHEREAS, Daniel ("Dan") Earl Williams, the former
2 representative, departed this life on October 23, 2025, at the age of 78;
3 and
4 WHEREAS, Representative Williams was born on April 15, 1947,
5 in Denver, Colorado, to Dale and Margaret Williams; and
6 WHEREAS, Representative Williams began his education in
7 Colorado's Eagle Valley, attended the University of Northern Colorado
8 for a brief period, and later attended the John F. Kennedy School of
9 Government, Harvard University, in 1988 to refine his political instincts;
10 and
11 WHEREAS, Representative Williams began his career in
12 agriculture as a partner with his father on the Williams Ranch in Edwards,
13 Colorado, later purchasing the ranch from his father and operating it as
14 a successful cattle and sheep operation; and
15 WHEREAS, Representative Williams was the father of two
