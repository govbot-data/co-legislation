MEMORIALIZING FORMER REPRESENTATIVE DAN
WILLIAMS.
WHEREAS, Daniel ("Dan") Earl Williams, the former representative,
departed this life on October 23, 2025, at the age of 78; and
WHEREAS, Representative Williams was born on April 15, 194 7, in
Denver, Colorado, to Dale and Margaret Williams; and
WHEREAS, Representative Williams began his education in
Colorado's Eagle Valley, attended the University of Northern Colorado
for a brief period, and later attended the John F. Kennedy School of
Government, Harvard University, in 1988 to refine his political instincts;
and
WHEREAS, Representative Williams began his career in agriculture
as a partner with his father on the Williams Ranch in Edwards, Colorado,
later purchasing the ranch from his father and operating it as a successful
cattle and sheep operation; and

WHEREAS, Representative Williams was the father of two children,
Jeff and Dana, who were raised on the family's ranch; and
WHEREAS, Representative Williams was a member of the Eagle
County Wool Growers Association, the Colorado Wool Growers
Association, the Colorado Cattlemen's Association, and the Colorado
Farm Bureau; and
WHEREAS, Not only was Representative Williams a good steward
of the land and the animals he raised, he was a man of incredible integrity
· and demonstrated a keen knowledge of issues impacting Colorado
ranchers and business owners. He believed in a life of public service and
valued his family. Many called him a dear friend and confidant; and
WHEREAS, Representative Williams was elected as Eagle County
Commissioner in 1976 and served two four-year terms; and
WHEREAS, Representative Williams was elected to the Colorado
House of Representatives in 1984 and served four two-year terms
representing House District 56, including Routt, Moffat, Eagle, Jackson,
and Grand counties; and
WHEREAS, As a member of the Colorado House of Representatives,
Representative Williams served as chair of the House Transportation and
Energy Committee; chair of the House Agriculture, Livestock, and
Natural Resources Committee; vice chair of the House State Affairs
Committee; chair of the Interim Legislative Committee on Water Quality
and Water Resources; and chair of the Highway Legislative Review
Committee; and
WHEREAS, Representative Williams was also appointed to the State
Board of Equalization and served on the Colorado Governor's AIDS
Council; and
WHEREAS, During his time in the legislature, Representative
Williams supported the interests of many western slope communities,
championing water and land use rights; oil and gas interests; and
transportation issues; and

WHEREAS, Representative Williams was a member of the Roundup
Riders of the Rockies and named 1985 Colt of the Year. The Town of
Avon honored Representative Williams as a "Citizen of the Valley" with
a bronze portrait, he served as a trustee on the board of the National
W estem Stock Show Association, and, in 2019, he was inducted into the
Colorado FFA Agricultural Hall of Fame; and
WHEREAS, After leaving the legislature, Representative Williams
applied his political and legislative experience as a partner in a
government relations consulting business from 1993 to 2019 with Stan
Sours, Laura Locke, and his partner, Bob Simpson, representing the
Colorado Cattlemen's Association, Colorado Wool Growers Association,
Colorado Beer Distributors Association, Colorado Farm Credit, Colorado
River Water Conservation District, Williams Energy Pipeline Midstream,
Mile High Racing and Entertainment, and many other clients along the
way; and
WHEREAS, Representative Williams, whether as an elected official
or lobbyist, was known by his colleagues and friends as a fierce advocate
for Colorado's agriculture industry; and
WHEREAS, Representative Williams was known to be fair and
honest. He knew when to compromise and was recognized as an effective
legislator and lobbyist by his constituents, clients, and colleagues; and
WHEREAS, Representative Williams was a mentor to a number of
individuals who have made their careers in politics, instilling in them the
need to work honestly, maintain integrity, and always respect the
institution of the General Assembly and the legislative process; and
WHEREAS, At the time of his death, Representative Williams was
living in Scottsdale, Arizona, enjoying a well-deserved retirement and his
passions for travel and music; and
WHEREAS, Representative Williams is survived by his partner, Bob
Simpson; his two children, Jeff Williams and Dana Williams; his sister,
Donna Parker; and his cousin, Sue Tolvo; now, therefore,
