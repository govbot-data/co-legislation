CONCERNING THE RECOGNITION OF DISTRICT WILDLIFE
MANAGER BOB HOLDER FOR HIS FIFTY YEARS OF
EXEMPLARY SERVICE TO THE STATE OF COLORADO.
WHEREAS, Bob Holder began his legendary career with the
Colorado Division of Wildlife in 1975 and was assigned to the Trinidad
West District in 1976, where he has spent the entirety of his 51-year
career protecting the wildlife resources of southern Colorado; and
WHEREAS, Known by generations of youth as "Mr. Bob Holder",
Bob has served as a mentor and hero to countless students, using a
captivating communication style and intimate subject matter knowledge
to inspire conservation ethics in people of all ages; and
WHEREAS, Bob Holder is particularly esteemed for his deep passion
for Colorado's black bears, delivering renowned bear education programs
and working tirelessly to resolve human-bear conflicts; and

WHEREAS, Bob's technical contributions to wildlife management are
historic, including trapping and transferring over 850 Merriam's turkeys
throughout Colorado and Utah and assisting in critical research by
trapping 240 birds for the state's wild turkey research program; and
WHEREAS, Through his exceptional ability to build landowner
relationships, Bob was instrumental in the acquisition of the 30,000-acre
Bosque del Oso State Wildlife Area (SWA), Colorado's largest SWA, and
has helped protect over 78,000 additional acres of critical habitat through
perpetual conservation easements; and
WHEREAS, In his role as a wildlife officer, Bob is known for his
"straight-shooter" communication style and his "sixth sense" that led to
the apprehension of hundreds of poachers, earning him a reputation for
being determined, aggressive, and fair; and
WHEREAS, Bob Holder's extraordinary career has been recognized
with the John D. Hart Wildlife Officer of the Year Award in 1996, the
North American Wildlife Officer of the Year Award in 2002, the National
Wild Turkey Federation National Enforcement Officer of the Year Award
in 2006, and the proclamation of June 10 as "Bob Holder Day" in 2018
in Las Animas County; and
WHEREAS, Bob Holder officially retired on March 1, 2026, marking
a half-century of dedicated service to the people of the state of Colorado;
now, therefore,
Be It Resolved by the House of Representatives of the Seventy-fifth
General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
That we, the members of the Colorado House of Representatives,
hereby extend our deepest gratitude to Bob Holder for his 50 years of
unwavering dedication to Colorado's wildlife and congratulate him on a
well-deserved retirement.
