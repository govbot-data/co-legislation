CONCERNING PERMISSIBLE METHODS FOR THE SALE OF LOTTERY TICKETS.
Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
SECTION 1. In Colorado Revised Statutes, 44-40-107, add (12)
as follows:
44-40-107. Licenses.
(12) LOTTERY TICKETS OR INSTANT SCRATCH GAME TICKETS THAT
ARE PRODUCED BY THE LOTTERY FOR SALE TO THE PUBLIC, EITHER DIRECTLY
OR THROUGH THE LICENSEE, MUST NOT BE SOLD ON CREDIT.
SECTION 2. In Colorado Revised Statutes, 44-40-109, amend
(2)(i), (3)(a)(II), and (3)(a)(III); and add (2)(j) and (3)(a)(IV) as follows:
________

44-40-109. Commission - powers and duties - rules.
(2) Except as provided in subsection (3) of this section, rules
promulgated pursuant to subsection (1) of this section must include:
(i) The manner in which lottery sales revenues are to be collected;
AND
(j) A PROHIBITION ON THE LOTTERY OR A LICENSEE'S SALE OF ANY
LOTTERY TICKETS PRODUCED BY THE LOTTERY FOR SALE TO THE PUBLIC,
DIRECTLY OR THROUGH THE LICENSEE, ON CREDIT.
(3) (a) The commission shall promulgate rules pursuant to
subsection (1) of this section for the general administration of all instant
scratch games. The rules must include:
(II) The method of paying prizes on winning instant scratch game
tickets; and
(III) The manner and amount of compensation, if any, to be paid to
licensed sales agents necessary to provide for the adequate availability of
instant scratch game tickets to prospective buyers and for the convenience
of the public; AND
(IV) A PROHIBITION ON THE SALE OF INSTANT SCRATCH GAME
TICKETS PRODUCED BY THE LOTTERY FOR SALE TO THE PUBLIC, EITHER
DIRECTLY OR THROUGH THE LICENSEE, ON CREDIT.
