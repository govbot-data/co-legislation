CONCERNING INCREASING SUPPORT FOR OLDER ADULTS IN THE WORKFORCE.
Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
SECTION 1. In Colorado Revised Statutes, 24-46.3-100.3, add
(2.6) as follows:
24-46.3-100.3. Definitions.
As used in this article 46.3, unless the context otherwise requires:
(2.6) "OLDER ADULT" MEANS AN INDIVIDUAL WHO IS FIFTY-FIVE
YEARS OLD OR OLDER.

SECTION 2. In Colorado Revised Statutes, 24-46.3-101, add (2.1)
as follows:
24-46.3-101. State work force development council - creation -
membership - funding through gifts, grants, and donations.
(2.1) FOR APPOINTMENTS TO THE STATE COUNCIL, OR A SUCCESSOR
ENTITY, MADE ON OR AFTER THE EFFECTIVE DATE OF THIS SUBSECTION (2.1),
AT LEAST ONE MEMBER APPOINTED BY THE GOVERNOR PURSUANT TO
SUBSECTION (2)(d) OF THIS SECTION MUST BE AN INDIVIDUAL WHO:
(a) IS AN OLDER ADULT; AND
(b) EITHER IS ACTIVELY INVOLVED IN OR HAS INTEREST,
KNOWLEDGE, OR EXPERIENCE IN ADVOCATING FOR THE INTERESTS OF OLDER
ADULTS IN THE WORKFORCE.
SECTION 3. In Colorado Revised Statutes, add 24-46.3-107 as
follows:
24-46.3-107. Meet with Colorado commission on aging - collect
data - "SMART Act" hearing.
(1) (a) BEGINNING IN 2027, AND EACH YEAR THEREAFTER, STAFF OR
APPOINTED MEMBERS OF THE STATE COUNCIL SHALL MEET TWICE A YEAR
WITH THE COLORADO COMMISSION ON THE AGING, CREATED PURSUANT TO
SECTION 26-11-101, OR A WORKFORCE-RELATED SUBCOMMITTEE OF THE
COLORADO COMMISSION ON THE AGING, ESTABLISHED PURSUANT TO
SECTION 26-11-102.
(b) THE STATE COUNCIL SHALL INVITE REPRESENTATIVES OF ENTITIES
WITH DEMONSTRATED INTEREST OR EXPERIENCE IN WORKFORCE SERVICES
FOR OLDER ADULTS, INCLUDING COMMUNITY-BASED ORGANIZATIONS,
ADVOCACY GROUPS, WORKFORCE POLICY EXPERTS, AND NONPROFIT
ORGANIZATIONS, TO PARTICIPATE IN THE MEETINGS HELD PURSUANT TO
SUBSECTION (1)(a) OF THIS SECTION.
(2) THE STATE COUNCIL, THE COLORADO COMMISSION ON THE
AGING, AND ANY OTHER PARTICIPATING ENTITIES SHALL WORK
COLLABORATIVELY TO:

(a) PROMOTE WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT AND FOSTER
SELF-SUFFICIENCY FOR OLDER ADULTS IN THE STATE;
(b) EXAMINE ISSUES RELATED TO EMPLOYMENT ACCESS, TRAINING,
SUPPORT, AND SERVICES FOR OLDER ADULTS IN THE STATE;
(c) REVIEW DATA COLLECTED THROUGH EXISTING RESOURCES AND
PLATFORMS BY THE STATE COUNCIL OR THE COLORADO COMMISSION ON THE
AGING RELATED TO OLDER ADULTS IN THE WORKFORCE;
(d) DISCUSS AND DETERMINE WHETHER ADDITIONAL DATA SHOULD
BE COLLECTED BY THE STATE COUNCIL OR THE COLORADO COMMISSION ON
THE AGING REGARDING OLDER ADULTS IN THE WORKFORCE TO ASSIST IN
FURTHERING THE OBJECTIVES SET FORTH IN SUBSECTIONS (2)(a) AND (2)(b)
OF THIS SECTION; AND
(e) PROVIDE AN OVERVIEW TO THE DEPARTMENT AND THE
DEPARTMENT OF HUMAN SERVICES, ON OR BEFORE DECEMBER 1, 2027, AND
ON OR BEFORE DECEMBER 1 OF EACH YEAR THEREAFTER, OF THE DATA
COLLECTED PURSUANT TO THIS SUBSECTION (2).
(3) BEGINNING IN 2028, AND EACH YEAR THEREAFTER, THE
DEPARTMENT AND THE DEPARTMENT OF HUMAN SERVICES SHALL JOINTLY
SUBMIT A REPORT TO THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY COMPILING THE DATA
COLLECTED PURSUANT TO THIS SECTION AND SUMMARIZING THE STATE OF
WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT AND EMPLOYMENT ACCESS FOR OLDER ADULTS
IN THE STATE, AND THE DEPARTMENT SHALL SUMMARIZE THE REPORT IN ITS
PRESENTATION TO THE SENATE BUSINESS, LABOR, AND TECHNOLOGY
COMMITTEE AND THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES BUSINESS AFFAIRS AND
LABOR COMMITTEE, OR THEIR SUCCESSOR COMMITTEES, DURING THE
HEARINGS HELD PURSUANT TO THE "SMART ACT", PART 2 OF ARTICLE 7 OF
TITLE 2.
