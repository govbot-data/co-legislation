101 MEMORIALIZING FORMER REPRESENTATIVE CARL MILLER.
1 WHEREAS, Our respected former colleague, Carl Eugene Miller,
2 a past member of the Colorado House of Representatives, departed this
3 life on August 9, 2025, at the age of 86, in Leadville, Colorado; and
4 WHEREAS, Representative Miller was born in Leadville on
5 October 28, 1938, to Carl and Ruth Miller; and
6 WHEREAS, After graduating from Leadville High School with
7 the class of 1956, Representative Miller began working in various local
8 mines, including the Irene manganese mine, the Julia Fiske lead mine,
9 and the Helena gold mine; and
10 WHEREAS, Soon after, Representative Miller volunteered for the
11 draft and joined the United States Army in 1958; after completing basic
12 training, he served in Gelnhausen, Germany, until 1960, when he was
13 honorably discharged; and
14 WHEREAS, Representative Miller then returned to Leadville,

30 and the race's Legacy Foundation helps ensure donations benefit and

2 WHEREAS, Representative Miller also contributed to the
3 community through his work as a dedicated and deeply involved public
4 servant; for example, he served as a Lake County Commissioner for 12
5 years, from 1977 to 1989; he worked on the Lake County School Finance
6 and Scholarship Committees and contributed to many other civic and
7 county engagements, such as the Hospital Advisory Group, the Council
8 on Aging, the Historic Preservation Commission, and the 3rd
9 Congressional Military Academy Advisory Selection Committee; and he
10 sat as a past chairman of Colorado's Region 13 Council of Governments;
11 and
12 WHEREAS, After deciding to run for state government,
13 Representative Miller was elected to the House of Representatives in
14 1996, where from 1997 to 2004 he served 4 terms as the state
15 Representative for District 61 and later District 56, representing the
16 citizens of Chaffee, Eagle, Gunnison, Hinsdale, Lake, Mineral, Park,
17 Pitkin, Rio Grande, Saguache, Summit, and Teller counties over the
18 course of his time with the General Assembly; and
19 WHEREAS, While serving as a state Representative,
20 Representative Miller was a member of several House committees,
21 including the Agriculture, Livestock, and Natural Resources Committee;
22 the Local Government Committee; the Business Affairs and Labor
23 Committee; and the Information and Technology Committee; several joint
24 and statutory committees, including the Police Officers' and Firefighters'
25 Pension Reform Commission; the Committee on Legal Services; the
26 Legislative Audit Committee, as Vice-chair; the Capital Development
27 Committee; and the Water Resources Review Committee; and also
28 several interim committees, including the Water and Land Resources
29 Issues Committee, the Commuter Airlines Committee, the Need to
30 Conduct Fiscal Study of State and Local Governments Committee, and
31 the Task Force to Evaluate Health Care Needs (Rural and Small Group);
32 and

34 Colorado's natural resources, especially its waters, minerals, and wildlife;
35 advancing developments in technology; strengthening Colorado's
36 economy; and promoting education, particularly in rural Colorado; and
