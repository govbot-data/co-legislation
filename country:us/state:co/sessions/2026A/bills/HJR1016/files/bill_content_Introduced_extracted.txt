HOUSE JOINT RESOLUTION 26-1016
101 CONCERNING HONORING THE 10TH MOUNTAIN DIVISION OF
102 COLORADO.
1 WHEREAS, Tucked in a high mountain valley south of Minturn
2 in Eagle County, Colorado, Camp Hale was the home base for the
3 renowned World War II 10th Mountain Division, an elite U.S. Army
4 mountain warfare unit; and
5 WHEREAS, Comprised of thousands of troops who trained
6 intensely to acquire rock climbing, skiing, and other vital mountaineering
7 skills necessary to battle within harsh high-altitude winter conditions,
8 Camp Hale served as a specialized military training site to prepare
9 soldiers for combat against European Axis forces in mountainous and
10 arctic conditions during World War II; and

1 WHEREAS, The severe winters and rugged terrain at Camp Hale
2 helped prepare the 10th Mountain Division's skiing soldiers for their
3 brave battles in the northern Italian Alps and Apennines, including a
4 particular battle in February 1945 when thousands of 10th Mountain
5 Division soldiers silently scaled the steep Riva Ridge in Italy and
6 prevented Germans from using the precipice to survey U.S. forces below
7 and then, the next day, they joined a successful assault on Mount
8 Belvedere, an Axis stronghold, during which hundreds of 10th Mountain
9 Division soldiers perished; and
10 WHEREAS, The 10th Mountain Division soldiers fought tirelessly
11 in Italy and central Europe until Germany's final surrender in 1945 and
12 then returned to the United States, where the division was demobilized
13 and inactivated on November 20, 1945, at Camp Carson, Colorado; and
14 WHEREAS, The 10th Mountain Division soldiers who trained at
15 Camp Hale during World War II not only distinguished themselves
16 internationally for their bravery but also brought worldwide attention to
17 Colorado's unique climate and geography for specialized military training;
18 and
19 WHEREAS, The now-inactive 10th Mountain Division changed
20 from its original purpose and now reflects the unique military needs of
21 our country following World War II, and the legacy of the 10th Mountain
22 Division deserves protection and tribute; we, the Seventy-fifth General
23 Assembly, recognize the hard work and remarkable bravery needed to
24 protect the United States and its beleaguered World War II Allies from
25 enemy forces; and
26 WHEREAS, Many veterans of the 10th Mountain Division
27 dedicated the rest of their lives to the conservation of Camp Hale and the
28 Tenmile Range area after World War II, creating the 10th Mountain
29 Division Hut Association. The association works to preserve 30 huts,
30 which allow visitors to experience the land and conditions as the 10th
31 Mountain Division did over 80 years ago; and
32 WHEREAS, In a historic act that speaks to the 10th Mountain
33 Division's valuable role in American history, President Joseph R. Biden
34 Jr. used his powers granted pursuant to the federal Antiquities Act for the
35 first time during his administration to declare Camp Hale the Camp
36 Hale-Continental Divide National Monument on October 12, 2022,
37 thereby protecting both the natural environment and the state heritage
