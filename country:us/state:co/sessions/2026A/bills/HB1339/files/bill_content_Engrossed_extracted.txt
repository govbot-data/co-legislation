March 31 is currently known as "Cesar Chavez Day" and may be
voluntarily observed as a legal holiday. The bill changes this March 31
voluntary legal holiday to instead be known as "Farm Workers Day".

1 Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
2 SECTION 1. Legislative declaration. (1) The general assembly
3 finds and declares that:
4 (a) After years of discrimination and unfair labor practices, brave
5 farm workers and activists organized and marched hundreds of miles to
6 demand fair wages, dignity, and respect. In 1975, these efforts resulted in
7 the California "Agricultural Labor Relations Act", which was a
8 groundbreaking law protecting the right of farm workers to unionize.
9 (b) The Farm Workers Movement achieved:
10 (I) The first collective bargaining agreement between farm
11 workers and growers in the continental United States;
12 (II) The first union contracts requiring rest periods, clean drinking
13 water, hand washing facilities, and clothing protective against pesticide
14 exposure;
15 (III) The first ban on pesticide spraying while workers were in the
16 fields and the first ban on dichloro-diphenyl-trichloroethane, commonly
17 known as DDT, and other dangerous pesticides;
18 (IV) The first and only performing pension plan for retired farm
19 workers;
20 (V) The first union contracts regulating safety and sanitary
21 conditions in farm labor camps and banning discrimination in
22 employment and the sexual harassment of female workers;
23 (VI) The abolition of the use of "el cortito", the infamous
24 short-handled hoe that physically disabled generations of farm workers;
25 and
26 (VII) The extension of state coverage for unemployment,
27 disability, and workers' compensation to farm workers.

1 (2) The general assembly further finds and declares that:
2 (a) Given the deeply disturbing allegations of sexual abuse by
3 Cesar Chavez, the general assembly cannot, in good conscience, continue
4 to celebrate a state holiday that bears his name; and
5 (b) This decision is not about erasing history or diminishing the
6 contributions that have shaped Latino communities across the country. It
7 is about honoring the courage of the girls and women, including the
8 renowned labor leader and co-founder of the Farm Workers Movement,
9 Dolores Huerta, who suffered in silence for decades before bravely
10 speaking out.
11 (3) Therefore, it is appropriate to rename March 31 as "Farm
12 Workers Day" to honor the history of our farm workers and celebrate
13 their ongoing contributions to our communities, state, and country.
