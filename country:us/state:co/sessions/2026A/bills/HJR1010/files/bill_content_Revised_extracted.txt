1 WHEREAS, More than 58,000 members of the United States
2 Armed Forces, including 623 Coloradans, lost their lives in the Vietnam
3 War, and more than 300,000 Americans were wounded; in addition, more
4 than 1,500 members of the United States Armed Forces are listed as
5 prisoners of war or missing in action in the Vietnam War and remain
6 unaccounted for, including 24 Coloradans; and
7 WHEREAS, According to the United States Department of
8 Veterans Affairs, more than 6 million veterans of the United States
9 Armed Forces who served during the Vietnam era are still alive today,
10 including, as of 2020, an estimate of more than 116,000 Coloradans; and
11 WHEREAS, Formal acknowledgment of Vietnam War veterans
12 is as important now as ever, not only for the veterans who served but for
13 all citizens; bipartisan federal legislation signed in 2017 permanently
14 enshrines March 29 as National Vietnam War Veterans Day; and
15 WHEREAS, April 30, 2026, marks the 51st anniversary of the fall
16 of the South Vietnamese capital of Saigon to the North Vietnamese Army
17 and the effective end of the Vietnam War; now, therefore,
18 Be It Resolved by the House of Representatives of the Seventy-fifth
19 General Assembly of the State of Colorado, the Senate concurring herein:
20 That we, the members of the Colorado General Assembly, hereby:
21 (1) Honor the service and sacrifices of Colorado military veterans
22 who served in the Vietnam War alongside South Vietnamese soldiers;
23 (2) Recognize the challenges faced by many veterans, both while
24 serving in the Vietnam War and upon returning home;
25 (3) Express our appreciation for the fortitude of Colorado
26 veterans; their activism on behalf of fellow veterans; their patriotism;
27 their accomplishments and contributions; and the leadership they have
28 shown their families, their community organizations, and the state; and
29 (4) Encourage all Coloradans to remember the sacrifices made by
30 Vietnam War veterans.
31 Be It Further Resolved, That copies of this Joint Resolution be sent
32 to Maj. Gen. Robert Davis, the Adjutant General of Colorado and
