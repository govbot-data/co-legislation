CONCERNING THE DESIGNATION OF THE CONVENING
DATE FOR THE FIRST REGULAR SESSION OF THE
SEVENTY-SIXTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY AND THE ADDITION OF
A JOINT RULE ESTABLISHING DEADLINES THAT APPLY TO
THE SENATE AND HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES FOR THE
SESSION BASED ON THE CONVENING DATE.
WHEREAS, Section 2-2-303.5 (3), Colorado Revised Statutes,
authorizes the General Assembly to designate the convening date for
regular sessions by joint resolution; now, therefore,
Be It Resolved by the House of Representatives of the Seventy-fifth
General Assembly of the State of Colorado, the Senate concurring herein:
That, pursuant to section 2-2-303.5 (3), Colorado Revised Statutes, the
General Assembly designates Monday, January 11, 2027, as the
convening date for the First Regular Session of the Seventy-sixth General
Assembly.
Be It Further Resolved, That in the Joint Rules of the Senate and
House of Representatives, add Joint Rule No. 22B as follows:
22B. Alternative Deadline Schedule
(a) Applicability. THIS JOINT RULE 22B APPLIES TO THE FIRST REGULAR
SESSION OF THE SEVENTY-SIXTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY, INCLUDING ANY

DEADLINES RELATED TO THE SESSION THAT ARE PRIOR TO ITS
CONVENING DATE. JOINT RULES 23 AND 24 CONTINUE TO APPLY
DURING THE SESSION; EXCEPT THAT THIS JOINT RULE 22B SUPERSEDES
CONFLICTING PROVISIONS IN JOINT RULE 23 (a)(1) AND (a)(2) AND
JOINT RULE 24 (b)(1)(A) AND (c). TO THE EXTENT THAT A JOINT RULE
REFERS TO THE DEADLINES ESTABLISHED IN JOINT RULE 23 (a)(1), THE
REFERENCE INSTEAD APPLIES TO THE DEADLINES SET FORTH IN
SUBSECTION (b)(1) OF THIS JOINT RULE 22B.
