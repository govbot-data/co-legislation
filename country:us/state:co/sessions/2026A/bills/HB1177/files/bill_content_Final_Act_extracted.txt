HOUSE BILL 26-1177
BY REPRESENTATIVE(S) Brown and Taggart, Sirota, McCluskie;
also SENATOR(S) Amabile and Kirkmeyer, Bridges.
CONCERNING ENDING WAGE ENHANCEMENT SUPPLEMENTAL PAYMENTS TO
NURSING HOME PROVIDERS, AND, IN CONNECTION THEREWITH,
REDUCING AN APPROPRIATION.
Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
SECTION 1. In Colorado Revised Statutes, 25.5-6-208, add (6.5)
as follows:
25.5-6-208. Nursing facility provider reimbursement - rules -
definition - repeal.
(6.5) NOTWITHSTANDING ANY OTHER PROVISION OF THIS SECTION TO
THE CONTRARY, THE STATE DEPARTMENT SHALL NOT MAKE A WAGE
ENHANCEMENT SUPPLEMENTAL PAYMENT TO AN ELIGIBLE NURSING HOME
PROVIDER ON OR AFTER THE EFFECTIVE DATE OF THIS SUBSECTION (6.5)
REGARDLESS OF WHEN THE SERVICES FOR WHICH WAGE ENHANCEMENT
SUPPLEMENTAL PAYMENTS ARE PROVIDED ARE DELIVERED.
