A BILL FOR AN ACT
101 CONCERNING A PROHIBITION ON THE USE OF VENTILATION SHUTDOWN
102 IN THE DEPOPULATION OF POULTRY.
Bill Summary
(Note: This summary applies to this bill as introduced and does
not reflect any amendments that may be subsequently adopted. If this bill
passes third reading in the house of introduction, a bill summary that
applies to the reengrossed version of this bill will be available at
http://leg.colorado.gov.)
Under current law, poultry owners may be required to destroy or
depopulate their entire flock of poultry when one of their birds tests
positive for a disease. Effective July 1, 2029, the bill prohibits an owner
of poultry from using ventilation shutdown and ventilation shutdown
plus, which methods kill poultry by gradually inducing fatal hyperthermia
or heat stroke, as methods of depopulating their poultry.

6 (1) THE SHORT TITLE OF THIS SECTION IS THE "MODERNIZING

8 (2) THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY:
9 (a) FINDS THAT WHEN AN OWNER OF POULTRY DEPOPULATES THEIR
10 ENTIRE FLOCK OF POULTRY IN RESPONSE TO A DISEASE, THE OWNER MUST
11 CARRY OUT THE DEPOPULATION AS HUMANELY AS POSSIBLE; AND
12 (b) OPPOSES THE USE OF VENTILATION SHUTDOWN AND
13 VENTILATION SHUTDOWN PLUS, BOTH OF WHICH KILL POULTRY BY
14 GRADUALLY INDUCING FATAL HYPERTHERMIA OR HEAT STROKE, IN FAVOR
15 OF HIGHER WELFARE DEPOPULATION METHODS.
16 (3) AS USED IN THIS SECTION, UNLESS THE CONTEXT OTHERWISE
17 REQUIRES:
18 (a) "DEPOPULATION" MEANS THE MASS DESTRUCTION OF POULTRY
19 FOR DISEASE CONTROL PURPOSES.
20 (b) "POULTRY" MEANS ANY DOMESTICATED BIRD, INCLUDING
21 CHICKENS, TURKEYS, DUCKS, GEESE, GUINEAS, SQUABS, OR PHEASANTS.
22 (c) "VENTILATION SHUTDOWN" MEANS A METHOD OF
23 DEPOPULATION THAT INVOLVES SEALING A BUILDING IN WHICH POULTRY
24 ARE CONFINED, SHUTTING INLETS, AND TURNING OFF FANS OR
25 VENTILATION SYSTEMS IN ORDER TO RAISE THE TEMPERATURE IN THE
26 BUILDING AND INDUCE DEATH OF THE POULTRY THROUGH FATAL
