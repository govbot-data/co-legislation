SENATE RESOLUTION 26-009
101 CONCERNING ALS AWARENESS MONTH.
1 WHEREAS, Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis, also commonly known
2 as Lou Gehrig's disease or ALS, is a progressive, fatal neurodegenerative
3 disease in which a person's brain loses connection with their muscles,
4 slowly reducing a person's ability to walk, talk, eat, and eventually
5 breathe; and
6 WHEREAS, Thousands of new ALS cases are reported every year,
7 and estimates show that every 90 minutes, someone is diagnosed with
8 ALS and someone passes away from ALS; and
9 WHEREAS, On average, patients diagnosed with ALS survive
10 only two to five years from the time of diagnosis; and
11 WHEREAS, The exact causes of ALS are unknown, although a
12 small percentage of cases are hereditary, and there is no known cure for
13 ALS; and
14 WHEREAS, People who have served in the military are more
15 likely to develop ALS and die from the disease than those with no history

2 WHEREAS, Securing access to new therapies, durable medical

5 WHEREAS, Clinical trials play a pivotal role in evaluating new

9 by providing comprehensive care for patients with ALS. Clinic care

16 WHEREAS, The ALS United Rocky Mountain organization

24 WHEREAS, ALS Awareness Month provides an opportunity to
25 increase public awareness of the dire circumstances of people living with
26 ALS and acknowledge the terrible impact this disease has on those
27 individuals and their families. Most importantly, it can help provide
28 support for research to eradicate this disease and for multidisciplinary
29 ALS clinics to administer comprehensive care for patients with ALS; and
30 WHEREAS, Patients with ALS have incredible courage and
