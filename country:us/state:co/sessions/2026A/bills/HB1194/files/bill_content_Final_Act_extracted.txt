SECTION 4. In Colorado Revised Statutes, amend 12-110-102 as
follows:
12-110-102. Legislative declaration.
(1) The general assembly hereby finds, determines, and declares that
the federal "Professional Boxing Safety Act of 1996", 15 U.S.C. sec. 6301
et seq., as amended, requires the state of Colorado to establish a state
boxing commission. Because there is no state boxing commission, any
professional boxing match held in Colorado has to be supervised by another
state's boxing commission, using safety guidelines and procedures
implemented by that state THE STATE HAS THEREFORE CREATED THE
COLORADO COMBATIVE SPORTS COMMISSION.
(2) The general assembly further finds and declares that it is in the
PAGE 2-HOUSE BILL 26-1194

best interests of the residents of Colorado, professional boxing COMBATIVE
SPORTS participants, and the future of the sport of boxing COMBATIVE
SPORTS in Colorado that the conduct of the sport COMBATIVE SPORTS be
subject to an effective and efficient system of strict control designed by the
general assembly. The system shall, at a minimum:
(a) Protect the safety of the participants; and
(b) Promote the public trust and confidence in the conduct of
professional boxing COMBATIVE SPORTS.
(3) To further public confidence and trust, this article 110 and rules
promulgated ADOPTED pursuant to this article 110 shall regulate all persons,
practices, and associations that relate to the operation of live professional
boxing COMBATIVE SPORTS events, performances, or contests held in
Colorado.
