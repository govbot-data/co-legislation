SECTION 3. Effective date. This act takes effect upon passage;
except that section 2 of this act takes effect only if House Bill 26-1410
becomes law, in which case section 2 takes effect upon the effective date
of this act or House Bill 26-1410, whichever is later.
SECTION 4. Safety clause. The general assembly finds,
determines, and declares that this act is necessary for the immediate
preservation of the public peace, health, or safety or for appropriations for
the support and maintenance of the departments of the state and state
institutions.
