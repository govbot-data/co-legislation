*The Chief Clerk of the House of Representatives and the Secretary of
the Senate are authorized to hire Legislative Aides pursuant to Joint Rule
39 of the Senate and the House of Representatives. If Legislative Aides
are authorized by the Executive Committee, acting in accordance with
such Joint Rule 39, to start prior to the convening of the next regular
session, they may be paid at the rate specified in this resolution from the
date they are authorized to start.
(2) That the Chief Clerk of the House of Representatives and the
Secretary of the Senate remain in such capacity after adjournment of the
General Assembly sine die until the convening of the First Regular
Session of the Seventy-sixth General Assembly in January 2027.
(3) That the Chair of the House Services Committee, with the
authorization of the Speaker of the House of Representatives, and the
President of the Senate are hereby separately authorized to retain and hire
replacements for employees referred to in subsection (1) of this resolution

for such period of time in the interim between the Second Regular
Session of the Seventy-fifth General Assembly and the First Regular
Session of the Seventy-sixth General Assembly as they deem necessary
for their respective houses.
(4) That the Chair of the House Services Committee, with the
authorization of the Speaker of the House of Representatives, and the
President of the Senate are hereby separately authorized to hire such
session-only employees for the next regular session as they deem
necessary for their respective houses and who may start their employment
on the date authorized.
