CONCERNING GRADUATE ACADEMIC REQUIREMENTS FOR A LICENSED
SCHOOL COUNSELOR, AND, IN CONNECTION THEREWITH,
ESTABLISHING A MINIMUM NUMBER OF CREDIT HOURS REQUIRED FOR
SPECIAL SERVICES LICENSURE WITH A SCHOOL COUNSELOR
ENDORSEMENT.
Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
SECTION 1. Legislative declaration. (1) The general assembly
finds and declares that:
(a) Schools play a critical role in preparing students for academic
achievement, postsecondary education, and participation in the workforce;
(b) Students' ability to succeed academically is often influenced by
personal, developmental, and social challenges that may affect school

engagement and learning;
(c) The social, emotional, and behavioral health needs of students
in Colorado schools have increased significantly in recent years. Failing to
address these needs affects students' academic success, school attendance,
safety, and long-term well-being.
(d) School counselors provide essential services to students and
schools by identifying and confronting obstacles to student learning and
supporting students with social and emotional development, academic
planning, and career and postsecondary readiness;
(e) In many schools, particularly in rural and smaller districts and
underserved communities, a licensed school counselor is often the only
readily available professional whose preparation includes training in
counseling practices and student development;
(f) School counselors are increasingly called upon to perform risk
and safety assessments, provide short-term counseling and crisis
intervention when tragedy strikes a school, coordinate services with families
and community providers, and support students experiencing trauma and
other behavioral health challenges;
(g) Because of these responsibilities, it is important that individuals
seeking licensure as school counselors receive comprehensive
graduate-level preparation that includes coursework in counseling practices,
student development, and strategies to help students experiencing stress,
behavioral challenges, or other barriers to academic success;
(h) National standards for counselor preparation, including those
established by recognized accrediting bodies, typically require graduate
programs of at least sixty semester credit hours and include coursework
related to mental health counseling, assessment, and crisis response; and
(i) Establishing minimum graduate credit requirements will help
ensure that school counselors entering Colorado schools possess the
knowledge and skills necessary to support students effectively.
(2) Therefore, the general assembly declares that establishing
minimum graduate-level preparation requirements for licensed school

(1) The department of education is designated as the sole agency

persons of good moral character:
(a) Initial special services license.
(I) The department of education, in its discretion, may issue an
initial special services license to any applicant who:
(B) Has met the standards of the state board of education concerning
academic and professional preparation, and experience, and performance,
as appropriate for the subject matter area or areas for which such THE initial
special services license is to be endorsed; EXCEPT THAT, TO QUALIFY FOR
SPECIAL SERVICES LICENSURE WITH A SCHOOL COUNSELOR ENDORSEMENT,

EDUCATION, A MASTER'S DEGREE OR HIGHER IN SCHOOL COUNSELING THAT
