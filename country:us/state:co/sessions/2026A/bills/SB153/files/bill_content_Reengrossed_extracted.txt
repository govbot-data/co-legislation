1 Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
2 SECTION 1. Legislative declaration. (1) The general assembly
3 finds and declares that:
4 (a) Schools play a critical role in preparing students for academic
5 achievement, postsecondary education, and participation in the
6 workforce;
7 (b) Students' ability to succeed academically is often influenced
8 by personal, developmental, and social challenges that may affect school
9 engagement and learning;
10 (c) The social, emotional, and behavioral health needs of students
11 in Colorado schools have increased significantly in recent years. Failing
12 to address these needs affects students' academic success, school
13 attendance, safety, and long-term well-being.
14 (d) School counselors provide essential services to students and
15 schools by identifying and confronting obstacles to student learning and
16 supporting students with social and emotional development, academic
17 planning, and career and postsecondary readiness;
18 (e) In many schools, particularly in rural and smaller districts and
19 underserved communities, a licensed school counselor is often the only
20 readily available professional whose preparation includes training in
21 counseling practices and student development;
22 (f) School counselors are increasingly called upon to perform risk
23 and safety assessments, provide short-term counseling and crisis
24 intervention when tragedy strikes a school, coordinate services with

1 families and community providers, and support students experiencing
2 trauma and other behavioral health challenges;
3 (g) Because of these responsibilities, it is important that
4 individuals seeking licensure as school counselors receive comprehensive
5 graduate-level preparation that includes coursework in counseling
6 practices, student development, and strategies to help students
7 experiencing stress, behavioral challenges, or other barriers to academic
8 success;
9 (h) National standards for counselor preparation, including those
10 established by recognized accrediting bodies, typically require graduate
11 programs of at least sixty semester credit hours and include coursework
12 related to mental health counseling, assessment, and crisis response; and
13 (i) Establishing minimum graduate credit requirements will help
14 ensure that school counselors entering Colorado schools possess the
15 knowledge and skills necessary to support students effectively.
16 (2) Therefore, the general assembly declares that establishing
17 minimum graduate-level preparation requirements for licensed school
18 counselors is necessary to provide effective student services, promote safe
19 and supportive learning environments, and ensure that students across
20 Colorado have access to well-prepared school counseling professionals.
