Bill Summary
(Note: This summary applies to this bill as introduced and does
not reflect any amendments that may be subsequently adopted. If this bill
passes third reading in the house of introduction, a bill summary that
applies to the reengrossed version of this bill will be available at
http://leg.colorado.gov.)
The bill requires the department of education to:
! Ensure that standardized summative assessments are
administered to students to the minimum extent possible;
and
! Apply for a federal waiver for federal assessment
requirements, if necessary.

1 Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
2 SECTION 1. In Colorado Revised Statutes, add 22-7-1020 as
3 follows:
4 22-7-1020. Cap administration of standardized summative
5 assessments - federal waiver - definition.
6 (1) AS USED IN THIS SECTION, UNLESS THE CONTEXT OTHERWISE
7 REQUIRES, "SUMMATIVE ASSESSMENT" MEANS AN ASSESSMENT THAT
8 MEASURES LEARNING ACCUMULATED BY A STUDENT THROUGH A COURSE
9 OF STUDY OVER A SEMESTER OR MORE AS DEMONSTRATED BY THE
10 STUDENT'S KNOWLEDGE AND SKILLS.
11 (2) TO THE EXTENT PERMITTED BY FEDERAL LAW, THE
12 DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION SHALL ENSURE THAT STANDARDIZED
13 SUMMATIVE ASSESSMENTS ARE ADMINISTERED TO THE MINIMUM EXTENT
14 PRACTICABLE WHILE STILL APPROPRIATELY AND EFFECTIVELY ASSESSING
15 THE ACADEMIC ACHIEVEMENT OF STUDENTS.
16 (3) PURSUANT TO 20 U.S.C. SEC. 7861, THE DEPARTMENT OF
17 EDUCATION SHALL, IF NECESSARY TO ADMINISTER SUMMATIVE
18 ASSESSMENTS TO THE MINIMUM EXTENT PRACTICABLE AS DESCRIBED IN
19 SUBSECTION (2) OF THIS SECTION, APPLY TO THE FEDERAL DEPARTMENT OF
20 EDUCATION FOR A WAIVER OF STATE ASSESSMENT REQUIREMENTS
21 PURSUANT TO 20 U.S.C. SEC. 6311 WITHIN NINETY DAYS AFTER THE
22 EFFECTIVE DATE OF THIS SECTION. THE APPLICATION MAY BE FOR A
23 STRATEGIC WAIVER THAT PROPOSES A SHORTENED ASSESSMENT, A
24 SAMPLING MODEL OF ASSESSMENT, A MATRIX SAMPLING MODEL OF
25 ASSESSMENT, OR ANY OTHER STRATEGY IDENTIFIED BY THE DEPARTMENT
26 OF EDUCATION.
