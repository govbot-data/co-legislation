2 WHEREAS, Caregiving youth often perform adult-level
3 responsibilities, including personal care, medication management,
4 household tasks, emotional support, and care coordination, frequently
5 without formal recognition or support; and
6 WHEREAS, Caregiving youth are at increased risk of academic
7 challenges, absenteeism, stress, anxiety, substance misuse, and social
8 isolation, yet remain largely underidentified and underserved within
9 education, health care, and social service systems; and
10 WHEREAS, Caregiving youth contribute significantly to the
11 stability and well-being of their families and communities, often at great
12 personal sacrifice and must navigate difficult situations that are no fault
13 of their own; and
14 WHEREAS, Increasing awareness of caregiving youth is essential
15 to promoting early identification, appropriate accommodations,
16 trauma-informed supports, and improved outcomes for these young
17 people; and
18 WHEREAS, February 20, 2026, is designated as Caregiving Youth
19 Day, and Colorado will formally observe Caregiving Youth Day on
20 February 27, 2026, at the Colorado state capitol; now, therefore,
21 Be It Resolved by the House of Representatives of the Seventy-fifth
22 General Assembly of the State of Colorado, the Senate concurring herein:
23
24 (1) That February 20, 2026, is hereby recognized as Caregiving
25 Youth Day in Colorado, with official observance taking place on
26 February 27, 2026;
27 (2) That the General Assembly honors and recognizes caregiving
28 youth across Colorado for their compassion, resilience, and contributions
29 to their families and communities;
30 (3) That the General Assembly honors and recognizes caregiving
31 youth as an underserved and underrecognized population deserving of
32 increased awareness, understanding, and support;
33 (4) That educators, school districts, health-care providers,
