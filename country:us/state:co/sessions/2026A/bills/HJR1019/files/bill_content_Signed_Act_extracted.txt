CONCERNING THE RECOGNITION OF CAREGIVING YOUTH
DAY.
WHEREAS, Caregiving youth are children and adolescents under
eighteen years old who provide primary or secondary care to a family
member or loved one of any age who has a chronic illness, frailty of
aging, disability, mental health condition, or other health-related need
requiring regular assistance; and
WHEREAS, It is estimated that more than six million caregiving
youth nationwide provide essential care while also balancing school,
extracurricular activities, employment, sibling care, and their own
physical, emotional, and social development; and
WHEREAS, The most recent data from the Healthy Kids Colorado
Survey shows that 12.3% of high schoolers and 20.4% of middle
schoolers identify as caregivers, with as many as 31.03 % of children
shouldering caregiving responsibilities in some districts; and

WHEREAS, Caregiving youth often perform adult-level
responsibilities, including personal care, medication management,
household tasks, emotional support, and care coordination, frequently
without formal recognition or support; and
WHEREAS, Caregiving youth are at increased risk of academic
challenges, absenteeism, stress, anxiety, substance misuse, and social
isolation, yet remain largely underidentified and underserved within
education, health care, and social service systems; and
WHEREAS, Care giving youth contribute significantly to the stability
and well-being of their families and communities, often at great personal
sacrifice and must navigate difficult. situations that are no fault of their
own; and
WHEREAS, Increasing awareness of caregiving youth is essential to
promoting early identification, appropriate accommodations,
trauma-informed supports, and improved outcomes for these young
people; and
WHEREAS, February 20, 2026, is designated as Caregiving Youth
Day, and Colorado will formally observe Caregiving Youth Day on
February 27, 2026, at the Colorado state capitol; now, therefore,
Be It Resolved by the House of Representatives of the Seventy-fifth
Genera/Assembly oft he State ofC olorado, the Senate concurring herein:
( 1) That February 20, 2026, is hereby recognized as Care giving Youth
Day in Colorado, with official observance taking place on February 27,
2026;
(2) That the General Assembly honors and recognizes caregiving
youth across Colorado for their compassion, resilience, and contributions
to their families and communities;
(3) That the General Assembly honors and recognizes care giving
youth as an underserved and underrecognized population deserving of
increased awareness, understanding, and support;
