2 WHEREAS, Farm workers are at the core of the Colorado
3 economy, with multiple industries relying on farm products to supply
4 food to everyone from local consumers to international markets; and
5 WHEREAS, Colorado farm workers now enjoy a range of rights,
6 but many people battled to secure those rights as they exist today.
7 Beginning in the 1960s, the hard work of attaining these rights was
8 pioneered by the National Farm Workers Association and the Agricultural
9 Workers Organizing Committee, which eventually merged to become the
10 United Farm Workers (UFW); and
11 WHEREAS, By 1970, the UFW had persuaded grape growers to
12 accept union contracts and had successfully organized workers across the
13 agricultural industry; and
14 WHEREAS, In 1975, the UFW's efforts resulted in the California
15 "Agricultural Labor Relations Act", a groundbreaking law protecting the
16 right of farm workers to unionize; and
17 WHEREAS, Workers at the UFW tirelessly devoted themselves
18 to making people aware of the struggles of farm workers and their need
19 for better pay and safer working conditions; and
20 WHEREAS, Celebrating farm workers is celebrating who we are,
21 what we eat, and the tireless work of the thousands of Coloradans whose
22 efforts make our food culture possible; now, therefore,
23 Be It Resolved by the House of Representatives of the Seventy-fifth
24 General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
25 That we, the members of the House of Representatives, recognize
26 March 31, 2026, as Farm Workers Day and honor the hard work, safety,
27 and dignity of Colorado's farm workers.
28 Be It Further Resolved, That copies of this Resolution be sent to
