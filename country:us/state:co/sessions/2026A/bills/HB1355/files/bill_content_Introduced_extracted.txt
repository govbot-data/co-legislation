Bill Summary
(Note: This summary applies to this bill as introduced and does
not reflect any amendments that may be subsequently adopted. If this bill

applies to the reengrossed version of this bill will be available at

Joint Budget Committee. The bill repeals the requirement for the
general assembly to appropriate money in the 2026-27 state fiscal year for
the out-of-school time program grant program (grant program). The bill
allows the general assembly to appropriate money for the grant program
at its discretion.
