CONCERNING MEASURES TO IMPROVE THE ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLICLY
FUNDED PROGRAMS AVAILABLE TO STUDENTS WHO ARE NOT
FULL-TIME IN-PERSON STUDENTS, AND, IN CONNECTION THEREWITH,
REQUIRING THE DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION TO REPORT ON ONLINE
AND PART-TIME ENRICHMENT EDUCATIONAL PROGRAMS AND
AUTHORIZING AN EXTENSION OF THE DESIGNATION OF A BOARD OF
COOPERATIVE SER VICES TO ADMINISTER THE STA TE WIDE
SUPPLEMENTAL ONLINE AND BLENDED LEARNING PROGRAM.

(1) AS USED IN THIS SECTION, UNLESS THE CONTEXT OTHERWISE
REQUIRES:
(a) "IN-PERSON SCHOOL" MEANS A SCHOOL WHERE STUDENTS
ATTEND IN PERSON ON THE SCHOOL CAMPUS FOR INSTRUCTION, WHICH

IN SECTION 22-30.7-102.
(g) "SUPPLEMENTAL PROGRAM" HAS THE MEANING SET FORTH IN

(2) ON OR BEFORE NOVEMBER 1, 2026, THE DEPARTMENT OF
EDUCATION SHALL SUBMIT A REPORT TO THE JOINT BUDGET COMMITTEE
REGARDING SINGLE-DISTRICT AND MULTI-DISTRICT ONLINE SCHOOLS AND
PART-TIME ENRICHMENT PROGRAMS.
(3) THE REPORT MUST INCLUDE THE FOLLOWING INFORMATION
PERTAINING TO ONLINE SCHOOLS, ONLINE PROGRAMS, AND SUPPLEMENTAL
ONLINE COURSES OFFERED BY IN-PERSON SCHOOLS:
(a) DESCRIPTIONS OF THE VARIOUS INSTRUCTIONAL AND FINANCIAL
MODELS THROUGH WHICH ONLINE SCHOOLS, ONLINE PROGRAMS, AND
SUPPLEMENTAL PROGRAMS ARE USED TO PROVIDE COURSE CREDIT IN K-12
PUBLIC EDUCATION, INCLUDING FULL-TIME ENROLLMENT, PART-TIME

ENROLLMENT, SUPPLEMENTAL COURSEWORK, AND CONCURRENT
ENROLLMENT;
(b) DATA ON THE NUMBER OF STUDENTS AND PERCENTAGE OF
STUDENTS RECEIVING ONLINE INSTRUCTION THROUGH ONLINE SCHOOLS,
ONLINE PROGRAMS, AND SUPPLEMENTAL PROGRAMS OFFERED BY IN-PERSON
SCHOOLS;
(c) COMMON COURSE DELIVERY METHODS, INCLUDING
SYNCHRONOUS AND ASYNCHRONOUS INSTRUCTION;
( d) COMMON CONTRACTUAL AND OTHER FINANCIAL ARRANGEMENTS
THROUGH WHICH COURSES ARE DELIVERED;
( e) THE ACADEMIC QUALITY OF ONLINE PROGRAMS AND THE
ACADEMIC PERFORMANCE OF STUDENTS ENROLLED IN ONLINE SCHOOLS,
ONLINE PROGRAMS, AND SUPPLEMENTAL PROGRAMS, IN COMPARISON TO
OTHER PUBLIC SCHOOL STUDENTS, TO THE EXTENT DATA IS AVAILABLE. THIS
MAY INCLUDE ANALYSIS OF DATA RELATED TO:
(I) STUDENT PARTICIPATION IN STA T E ASSESSMENTS, INCLUDING THE
NUMBER AND PERCENTA GE OF STUDENTS ENROLLED IN ONLINE SCHOOLS OR
ONLINE PROGRAMS WHO OPT OUT OF STA TE ASSESSMENTS, AS PERMITTED
UNDER STATE LAW;
(II) STATE ACCOUNTABILITY METRICS;
(III) STUDENT PERSISTENCE;
(IV) STUDENT POSTSECONDARY OUTCOMES, SUCH AS
MATRICULATION TO FOUR-YEAR INSTITUTIONS OR OTHER POSTSECONDARY
PATHWAYS; AND
(V) SUPPLEMENTAL DATA, SUCH AS STUDENT-TO-TEACHER RATIOS
IN ONLINE SCHOOLS, ONLINE PROGRAMS, AND SUPPLEMENTAL PROGRAMS;
(f) OPTIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR COLLECTING ADDITIONAL
DATA ON A TEMPORARY OR ONGOING BASIS TO ENHANCE LOCAL EDUCATION
PROVIDER, COMMUNITY, AND STATE BOARD OF EDUCATION VISIBILITY AND
OVERSIGHT OF ONLINE SCHOOL AND ONLINE PROGRAM QUALITY;

(g) A FISCAL ANALYSIS COMPARING STATE PAYMENT FOR ONLINE
SCHOOLS, ONLINE PROGRAMS, AND SUPPLEMENTA L PROGRAMS AGAINST
ACTUAL COSTS INCURRED. THE REPORT MAY INDICATE WHETHER
EXPENDITURES VARY SIGNIFICANTLY FOR DIFFERENT TYPES OF PROGRAMS.
(h) OPTIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR:
(I) OVERSIGHT STRUCTURES FOR ONLINE PROGRAMS THA TTA KE INTO
ACCOUNT THE OPERATIONAL REALITIES OF ONLINE EDUCATION WHILE
SUPPORTING HIGH-QUALITY OUTCOMES FOR STUDENTS; AND
(II) FUNDING CHANGES NECESSARY TO ADDRESS SIGNIFICANT
DISPARITIES BETWEEN PROGRAM EXPENDITURES AND PER PUPIL STA TE
PAYMENTS FOR ONLINE SCHOOLS, ONLINE PROGRAMS, AND SUPPLEMENTAL
PROGRAMS TO ENSURE APPROPRIATE USE OF PUBLIC RESOURCES.
(4) (a) To PROVIDE ADDITIONAL INFORMATION ON THE QUALITY OF
ONLINE PROGRAMS AND ONLINE SCHOOLS, LOCAL EDUCATION PROVIDERS
MAY SUBMIT SUPPLEMENTAL MATERIALS ABOUT ONLINE PROGRAMS AND
ONLINE SCHOOLS THAT INCLUDE, BUT ARE NOT LIMITED TO:
(I) INDIVIDUAL STUDENT ACADEMIC GROWTH MEASURED
YEAR-OVER-YEAR;
(II) INDIVIDUAL STUDENT PROGRESS TOWARD GRADUATION EACH
ACADEMIC YEAR;
(III) SCHOOL LEVEL PROGRAMS FOCUSED ON ONBOARDING, STUDENT
SUPPORT, AND LEARNER ENGAGEMENT;
(IV) HIGH SCHOOL PROGRAMMING DESIGNED TO EQUIP STUDENTS
FOR EMPLOYMENT, MILITARY ENLISTMENT, OR HIGHER EDUCATION; AND
(V) ALIGNMENT OF PROGRAM OFFERINGS TO NATIONAL STANDARDS
IN ONLINE LEARNING, SUCH AS NATIONAL STANDARDS FOR QUALITY ONLINE
PROGRAMS.
(b) TO PROVIDE ADDITIONAL INFORMATION ON THE COST OF ONLINE
PROGRAMS AND ONLINE SCHOOLS, LOCAL EDUCATION PROVIDERS MAY
SUBMIT ANALYSIS OF EXPENDITURES UNIQUE TO ONLINE SCHOOLS AND

STUDENTS;
( c) ADDRESS TO WHAT EXTENT THESE ACTIVITIES ARE ALIGNED TO
SUBJECTS IN WHICH STA TE STA ND ARDS EXIST;
(d) COMPARE FUNDED SEAT-TIME TO ACTUAL SEAT-TIME. IF THE

IN CORPORAT E REASONABLE ESTIMATES AND IDENTIFY OPTIONS FOR
IMPROVING DATA QUALITY.
(e) INCLUDE OPTIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR CURTAILING
PROGRAM COSTS AND IMPROVING PROGRAM OVERSIGHT TO ENSURE
