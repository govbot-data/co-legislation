SENATE RESOLUTION 26-005
BY SENATOR(S) Rich and Snyder, Amabile, Baisley, Benavidez, Bridges,

J., Hinrichsen, Kipp, Kirkmeyer, Kolker, Lindstedt, Liston, Mullica, Pelton B.,

Wilson, Coleman.
CONCERNING THE RECOGNITION OF SINGLE PARENT DAY.
WHEREAS, The greatest need of every child is to be loved; and
WHEREAS, Parents invest an immeasurable amount of time, energy,
and devotion to raise their children and to provide them with the support
and guidance required to becomes responsible, productive, and successful
adults; and
WHEREAS, The responsibility of raising children is particularly
difficult when it falls on the shoulders of a single parent, whether it be
due to divorce, death, or any circumstance; and
WHEREAS, According to United States Census data, almost a quarter
of United States families with children under 18 years old are
single-parent families, amounting to over 10 million households; and
WHEREAS, In 2023, over 15 million children were living in
single-parent families in the United States; and
WHEREAS, Single parents deserve respect and recognition for their
dedication and achievements; and
WHEREAS, In 1984, then-President Ronald Reagan declared March
21 as "National Single Parent Day"; now, therefore,

Be It Resolved by the Senate of the Seventy-fifth General Assembly of
the State of Colorado:
That we, the members of the Colorado Senate, continue to designate
March 21 as "Single Parent Day in Colorado" in respectful recognition of
single parents in Colorado.
Be It Further Resolved, That a copy of this Resolution be sent to
Janice S. Moglen, longtime Colorado advocate for Single Parent Day.
