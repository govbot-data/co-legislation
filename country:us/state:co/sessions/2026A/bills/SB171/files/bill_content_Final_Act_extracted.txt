SENATE BILL 26-171
BY SENATOR(S) Cutter and Wallace, Amabile, Exum, Jodeh, Coleman;
also REPRESENTATIVE(S) Lukens and Smith, Duran, Goldstein, Ricks,
Story, Titone, McCluskie.
CONCERNING PROHIBITING THE DISPOSAL OF PREPRODUCTION PLASTIC
MATERIALS AT A LOCATION THAT DOES NOT HAVE A CERTIFICATE OF
DESIGNATION TO OPERATE AS A SOLID WASTE DISPOSAL SITE AND
FACILITY.
Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
SECTION 1. In Colorado Revised Statutes, repeal as added by
Senate Bill 26-016 25-15-329.
SECTION 2. In Colorado Revised Statutes, add 30-20-125 as
follows:
30-20-125. Preproduction plastic materials - disposal
requirements - definition.
(1) AS USED IN THIS SECTION, UNLESS THE CONTEXT OTHERWISE
REQUIRES, "PREPRODUCTION PLASTIC MATERIALS" HAS THE MEANING SET
________
Capital letters or bold & italic numbers indicate new material added to existing law; dashes
through words or numbers indicate deletions from existing law and such material is not part of
the act.

FORTH IN SECTION 25-8-1102 (3).
(2) A PERSON SHALL NOT DISPOSE OF PREPRODUCTION PLASTIC
MATERIALS AT A LOCATION THAT HAS NOT BEEN ISSUED A CERTIFICATE OF
DESIGNATION PURSUANT TO SECTION 30-20-105.
SECTION 3. Act subject to petition - effective date. This act
takes effect August 12, 2027; except that, if a referendum petition is filed
pursuant to section 1 (3) of article V of the state constitution against this act
or an item, section, or part of this act within the ninety-day period after final
adjournment of the general assembly, then the act, item, section, or part will
