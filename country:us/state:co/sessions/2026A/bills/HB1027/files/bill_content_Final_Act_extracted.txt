CONCERNING BOARDS OF COOPERATIVE SERVICES, AND, IN CONNECTION
THEREWITH, PERMITTING A RETIREE TO WORK AS AN EXECUTIVE
DIRECTOR OF A BOARD OF COOPERATIVE SERVICES WITHOUT LOSING
RETIREMENT BENEFITS AND CLARIFYING BOARD OF COOPERATIVE
SERVICES ELIGIBILITY FOR POSTSECONDARY AND WORKFORCE
READINESS FUNDING.
Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Colorado:

(1.9)(a)(I), (1.9)(a)(II)(B), (1.9)(a)(II)(C), and (1.9)(h); and add (1.9)(k) as
follows:

definitions - repeal.
(1.9) (a) (I) Subject to subsection (1.9)(h) of this section, a service
