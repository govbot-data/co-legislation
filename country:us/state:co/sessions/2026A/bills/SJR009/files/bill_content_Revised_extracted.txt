1 WHEREAS, Children and adults go missing from their families for
2 a number of reasons: They run away or wander and become lost; they
3 disappear under circumstances indicating that their disappearance may
4 not have been voluntary; or they go missing under suspicious
5 circumstances, indicating that they may be in physical danger or the
6 victims of foul play; and
7 WHEREAS, In 2002, then Colorado Governor Bill Owens signed
8 House Bill 02-1083, establishing the AMBER (America's Missing:
9 Broadcast Emergency Response) Alert program in Colorado. Named after
10 nine-year-old Amber Hagerman, who was abducted and killed in Texas
11 in 1996, the program ensures that local law enforcement agencies can use
12 Colorado's Emergency Alert System to broadcast information to alert
13 citizens of missing children; and
14 WHEREAS, Since 1996, AMBER Alerts have successfully
15 recovered 1,221 children; and
16 WHEREAS, For all the important work done to recover missing
17 children, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children reported
18 that more than 32,000 missing child reports were made in 2025, and the
19 organization lists 42 missing Coloradan children who are from every area
20 of the state. Though all 42 disappeared from their families when they
21 were children, some have been missing for decades; and
22 WHEREAS, While too many families grieve and hope for their
23 missing children, others are in search of their missing family members of
24 all ages; and
25 WHEREAS, The awareness of citizens, media, and law
26 enforcement, as well as their support, their efforts, and their search for
27 missing persons, must continue; and
28 WHEREAS, The public plays a critical role in the success of the
29 AMBER and all other Alert programs, as they become the eyes and ears
30 of law enforcement and can be on the lookout for the missing person; and
31 WHEREAS, The common misconception that a person must be
32 absent for at least 24 hours before being legally classified as missing is
33 dangerous; when there is evidence of violence or of an unusual absence,
34 it is critical that an investigation begin promptly; and

1 WHEREAS, New advances in technology must be used and made

3 WHEREAS, The state of Colorado is committed to the safety and
4 well-being of its citizens, both children and adults; and
5 WHEREAS, The recognition of Missing Persons Day in Colorado
6 firmly embeds in our state calendar the importance of missing persons
7 and their families and friends and raises a level of awareness with other
8 cities and states that have also designated a Missing Persons Day; and
9 WHEREAS, The recognition of Missing Persons Day in Colorado
10 offers a platform to plan events that will connect families with the
11 resources they need to help identify and find their long-term missing
12 loved ones; and
13 WHEREAS, The recognition of Missing Persons Day in Colorado
14 ensures that the families and friends of the missing know that they are not
15 alone in this tragedy of uncertainties and lack of closure, which leads to
16 extreme suffering and long-lasting effects on the health, finances, and
17 relationships of those left behind; and
18 WHEREAS, Every missing person is someone's son or daughter,
19 father or mother, brother or sister, partner or friend, and every missing
20 person deserves to be searched for and found; now, therefore,
