HOUSE JOINT RESOLUTION 26-1029
101 CONCERNING INVESTIGATING ALLEGATIONS OF VIOLATIONS OF THE
102 "COLORADO FALSE CLAIMS ACT".
1 WHEREAS, Credible evidence exists that whistleblowers,
2 witnesses, and public officials within Colorado state and local
3 governments are being systematically intimidated and retaliated against
4 through the misuse of public resources and the select prosecution or
5 nonprosecution of the wrongdoers involved; and
6 WHEREAS, Examples of the systematic misconduct involved
7 include patterns of alleged corruption within the Colorado judicial
8 branch, alleged interference with the 2024 presidential election, publicly
9 funded self-investigations of various incidents of alleged official
10 misconduct, nondisclosure and hush money agreements valued at over $4
11 million negotiated between the Colorado Office of the Attorney General

7 WHEREAS, Under the "Colorado False Claims Act", a person is
8 liable to the state or a political subdivision of the state if the person
9 submits certain fraudulent claims to the state or political subdivision, and
10 the Attorney General or a private person may bring a civil action against
11 a person who made the fraudulent claim; and
12 WHEREAS, Because of conflicts of interests, the Colorado
13 Attorney General and the entire Colorado Department of Law are unable
14 to impartially enforce the "Colorado False Claims Act"; and
15 WHEREAS, Twenty-two other states and the District of Columbia
16 have their own equivalent civil false claims acts, including California,
17 Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Georgia, Hawaii, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa,
18 Kansas, Maryland, Massachusetts, Minnesota, Nevada, New Jersey, New
19 Mexico, New York, North Carolina, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee,
20 and Vermont; and
21 WHEREAS, The Denver office and Wyoming office of the United
22 States Department of Justice also have conflicts of interest related to
23 individuals involved in the alleged public corruption, and those conflicts
24 impede impartial investigations of the factual circumstances; now,
25 therefore,
26 Be It Resolved by the House of Representatives of the Seventy-fifth
27 General Assembly of the State of Colorado, the Senate concurring herein:
28 That we, the members of the General Assembly:
29 (1) Shall submit a formal request to the United States Department
30 of Justice to assign an office other than the Denver or Wyoming offices
31 to complete a conflict-free investigation of the alleged public corruption
32 within Colorado state and local governments;
