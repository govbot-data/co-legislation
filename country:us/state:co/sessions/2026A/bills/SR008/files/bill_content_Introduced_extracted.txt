SENATE RESOLUTION 26-008
101 CONCERNING THE RECOGNITION OF THE 125TH ANNIVERSARY OF
102 WESTERN COLORADO UNIVERSITY.
1 WHEREAS, Western Colorado University was founded in 1901
2 in Gunnison, Colorado, as the Colorado State Normal School, becoming
3 the first institution of higher education on Colorado's Western Slope; and
4 WHEREAS, For 125 years, Western Colorado University has
5 served the students, families, and communities of the Gunnison Valley
6 and the broader Western Slope, evolving from a small teachers' college
7 into a comprehensive public university offering undergraduate and
8 graduate programs across the arts, sciences, business, education, and
9 environmental studies; and
10 WHEREAS, The institution's evolution from Colorado State
11 Normal School to Western State College in 1923 and ultimately to
12 Western Colorado University reflects its expanding academic mission and
13 enduring commitment to the region it serves; and
14 WHEREAS, Western Colorado University plays a vital role in
15 advancing Colorado's workforce by preparing graduates in high-demand

30 (1) That the Colorado Senate hereby recognizes and celebrates the
