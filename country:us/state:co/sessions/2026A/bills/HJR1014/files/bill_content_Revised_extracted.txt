101 CONCERNING HONORING POST-9/11 VETERANS.
1 WHEREAS, Following the attacks on the World Trade Center on
2 September 11, 2001, the United States engaged in extended military
3 operations in Iraq and Afghanistan, spanning approximately two decades.
4 These operations included Operation Enduring Freedom, Operation
5 Freedom's Sentinel, Operation Iraqi Freedom, Operation New Dawn, and
6 Operation Inherent Resolve; and
7 WHEREAS, During this period, the United States deployed more
8 than two million American service members to Iraq and Afghanistan. Of
9 those service members, more than 6,900 were killed in action, including
10 107 service members from Colorado; and
11 WHEREAS, Of those service members who came home to
12 Colorado, many continue to live with post-traumatic stress disorder,
