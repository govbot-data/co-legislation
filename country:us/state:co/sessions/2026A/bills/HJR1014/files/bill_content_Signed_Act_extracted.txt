CONCERNING HONORING POST-9/11 VETERANS.
WHEREAS, Following the attacks on the World Trade Center on
September 11, 2001, the United States engaged in extended military
operations in Iraq and Afghanistan, spanning approximately two decades.
These operations included Operation Enduring Freedom, Operation
Freedom's Sentinel, Operation Iraqi Freedom, Operation New Dawn, and
Operation Inherent Resolve; and
WHEREAS, During this period, the United States deployed more than
two million American service members to Iraq and Afghanistan. Oft hose
service members, more than 6,900 were killed in action, including 107
service members from Colorado; and

WHEREAS, Of those service members who came home to Colorado,
many continue to live with post-traumatic stress disorder, traumatic brain
injuries, and other service-related conditions as a result of their
deployment to Iraq and Afghanistan that require ongoing care and
support; and
WHEREAS, The families and loved ones of service members also
share in those sacrifices and carry the burdens associated with combat;
and
WHEREAS, As the fifth anniversary of the withdrawal of United
States troops from Afghanistan nears, it is imperative to acknowledge the
sacrifices made by the veterans who served after September 11, 2001, as
many are just beginning to share their stories, process their grief, and
adjust to civilian life; now, therefore,
Be It Resolved by the House of Representatives of the Seventy-fifth
General Assembly oft he State ofC olorado, the Senate concurring herein:
(1) That we, the members of the Seventy-fifth General Assembly,
honor the courage, service, and sacrifice of all Americans who served in
military operations following September 11, 2001;
(2) That we solemnly remember the 107 service members of the
United States Armed Forces from Colorado who lost their lives during
this period of service;
(3) That we express profound gratitude to all Colorado veterans who
served during this time for their dedication and commitment; and
( 4) That we reaffirm the ongoing responsibility of the Colorado
General Assembly to ensure that veterans and their families receive
timely, comprehensive, and competent care, support, and recognition.
