103 MEMORY OF SGT. JOHN "JACK" THURMAN.
1 WHEREAS, Sergeant John "Jack" Ryland Thurman, United States
2 Marine Corps, was born on September 27, 1925, in South Dakota, and
3 passed February 1, 2025, at 99 years old, in Loveland; and
4 WHEREAS, Jack married the love of his life, Carol Jean Meier,
5 on October 22, 1951, and together, the couple raised four children; and
6 WHEREAS, After many decades together, Carol Jean passed June
7 22, 2009; and
8 WHEREAS, Sgt. Thurman was a veteran of World War II, a

1 survivor of Iwo Jima, and one of just a few select soldiers pictured in the
2 iconic "Gung Ho" photo of U.S. Marines atop Japan's Mt. Suribachi
3 mid-battle; and
4 WHEREAS, Jack enlisted on his 18th birthday with the United
5 States Marine Corps at Minnesota's Ft. Snelling; was selected for special
6 forces training at Marine Corps Base Camp Pendleton, CA, a group that
7 became known as the original "Carlson's Raiders", and was attached to
8 the 5th Marine Division 27th Regiment, 2nd Battalion, Foxtrot Company,
9 and Headquarters; and
10 WHEREAS, Sgt. Thurman deployed to the Japanese island of Iwo
11 Jima from February 19, 1945, to the end of March 1945, where he was in
12 the first waves of steel "amtracs" (amphibious assault vehicles) that
13 landed on Red Beach 1; and
14 WHEREAS, An advanced infantry sniper, Sgt. Thurman provided
15 coverage for the six original Iwo Jima flag raisers, saved numerous
16 Marines by securing pillboxes and Japanese tunnels, located a strategic
17 map identifying Japanese heavy artillery locations-which impacted the
18 outcome of the war, provided supplies to the front lines, and rescued
19 sailors and Marines from a downed B-29 bomber on Motoyama Airfield
20 No. 1 while under direct fire; and
21 WHEREAS, Sgt. Thurman took mortar wounds to his hip and face
22 during the battle, and his arms numbed from the impact of his M1
23 Carbine rifle itself taking a hit, but he was determined to keep moving
24 forward; and
25 WHEREAS, For his heroism, Sgt. Thurman received a Bronze Star
26 Medal, Good Conduct Medal, American Campaign Medal,
27 Asiatic-Pacific Campaign Medal, and the World War II Victory Medal,
28 in addition to an Honorable Discharge; and
29 WHEREAS, Sgt. Thurman's military memorabilia has been
30 preserved at museums such as the National Museum of the Marine Corps
31 near Quantico, VA; the World War II Memorial in Washington, D.C.; the
32 Center for American Values in Pueblo; and the Broomfield Veterans
33 Museum; and
34 WHEREAS, After World War II, Sgt. Thurman had a successful
35 career as an architect and designer in Colorado, best known for his

1 Be It Resolved by the Senate of the Seventy-fifth General Assembly
2 of the State of Colorado, the House of Representatives concurring herein:
3 (1) That the portion of U.S. Highway 34 between Wilson Ave. and
4 North County Rd. 23H be designated as the "Sgt. John 'Jack' Thurman
5 Memorial Highway";
6 (2) That the Colorado Department of Transportation may accept
7 and expend gifts, grants, and donations for the purpose of the initial
8 placement of signs to mark the portion of U.S. Highway 34 between
9 Wilson Ave. and North County Rd. 23H as the "Sgt. John 'Jack' Thurman
10 Memorial Highway"; and
11 (3) That the Colorado Department of Transportation may explore
12 a cooperative agreement with the City of Loveland, Larimer County, or
13 both, for the maintenance of the signs marking the "Sgt. John 'Jack'
14 Thurman Memorial Highway".
15 Be It Further Resolved, That copies of this Joint Resolution be sent
16 to Commander Karen Thurman, Larimer County Commissioner John
17 Kefalas, and Colorado Department of Transportation Executive Director
18 Shoshana Lew.
