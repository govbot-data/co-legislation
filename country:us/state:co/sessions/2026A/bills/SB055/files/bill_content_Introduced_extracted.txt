Bill Summary
(Note: This summary applies to this bill as introduced and does
not reflect any amendments that may be subsequently adopted. If this bill
passes third reading in the house of introduction, a bill summary that
applies to the reengrossed version of this bill will be available at
http://leg.colorado.gov.)
The bill creates a registry in the department of education for school
personnel who have been found to violate the school district's conduct
and discipline code or their employee contract.

16 SCHOOL PERSONNEL IN PUBLIC SCHOOLS WITH DISCIPLINARY RECORDS, AS

18 (e) "SCHOOL DISTRICT" HAS THE MEANING SET FORTH IN SECTION
19 22-55.5-102.
20 (f) "SCHOOL PERSONNEL" MEANS TEACHERS, ADMINISTRATORS,
21 SCHOOL RESOURCE OFFICERS, AND OTHER EMPLOYEES OF A SCHOOL.
22 (2) (a) THE DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION SHALL CREATE AND
23 MAINTAIN A COMPUTERIZED CENTRAL REGISTRY OF EACH DISCIPLINARY
24 RECORD OF SCHOOL PERSONNEL'S FOUND MISCONDUCT.
25 (b) A SCHOOL SHALL REGISTER ANY SCHOOL PERSONNEL,
26 PURSUANT TO THIS SECTION, WITHIN THIRTY DAYS AFTER DISCIPLINING
27 THE SCHOOL PERSONNEL FOR CONDUCT DESCRIBED IN SUBSECTION (2)(a)

12 (d) THE REGISTRY IS ONLY ACCESSIBLE TO ADMINISTRATORS, AND
