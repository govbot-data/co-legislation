CONCERNING PUBLISHING AN EASILY ACCESSIBLE LIST OF SECURE
TRANSPORTATION PROVIDERS ON CERTAIN DEPARTMENTS' WEBSITES.
Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
SECTION 1. In Colorado Revised Statutes, add 25.5-5-428 as
follows:
25.5-5-428. Secure transportation providers - accessible list.
THE STATE DEPARTMENT SHALL PUBLISH ON THE STATE
DEPARTMENT'S WEBSITE AN EASILY ACCESSIBLE LIST OF SECURE
TRANSPORTATION PROVIDERS, AS DEFINED IN SECTION 27-65-102, THAT
HAVE CONTRACTS WITH MANAGED CARE ENTITIES. THE LIST MUST INCLUDE
THE CONTACT INFORMATION FOR THE SECURE TRANSPORTATION PROVIDER,
INCLUDING AN ACTIVE PHONE NUMBER OR WEBSITE.
________
Capital letters or bold & italic numbers indicate new material added to existing law; dashes

SECTION 2. In Colorado Revised Statutes, 27-50-404, add (1)(d)
as follows:
27-50-404. Care coordination - responsibilities of behavioral
health administrative services organizations - coordination with
managed care entities.
(1) (d) THE BHA SHALL PUBLISH ON THE BHA'S WEBSITE AN EASILY
ACCESSIBLE LIST OF SECURE TRANSPORTATION PROVIDERS, AS DEFINED IN
SECTION 27-65-102, THAT HAVE CONTRACTS WITH BEHAVIORAL HEALTH
ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES ORGANIZATIONS. THE LIST MUST INCLUDE THE
CONTACT INFORMATION FOR THE SECURE TRANSPORTATION PROVIDER,
INCLUDING AN ACTIVE PHONE NUMBER OR WEBSITE.
SECTION 3. Act subject to petition - effective date. This act
takes effect at 12:01 a.m. on the day following the expiration of the
ninety-day period after final adjournment of the general assembly (August
12, 2026, if adjournment sine die is on May 13, 2026); except that, if a
referendum petition is filed pursuant to section 1 (3) of article V of the state
constitution against this act or an item, section, or part of this act within
such period, then the act, item, section, or part will not take effect unless
