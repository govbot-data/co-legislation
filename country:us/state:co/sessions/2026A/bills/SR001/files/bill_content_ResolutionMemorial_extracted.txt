SENATE RESOLUTION 26-001
BY SENATOR(S) Pelton B., Baisley, Bright, Carson, Frizell, Kirkmeyer,
Liston, Pelton R., Rich, Simpson, Zamora Wilson.
CONCERNING DEMANDING THE COLORADO DEPARTMENT
OF TRANSPORTATION TO IMPLEMENT ROAD IMPROVEMENT
PROJECTS IN MORGAN COUNTY.
WHEREAS, Pursuant to article 1 of title 43, Colorado Revised
Statutes, the Colorado Department of Transportation (CDOT) is charged
with the maintenance of the state's transportation system; and
WHEREAS, For years, CDOT has failed to take significant steps to
implement and fund maintenance projects for state highways in Morgan
County, and as a result, most of the state highways in Morgan County are
in poor condition; and
WHEREAS, Seventy percent of the approximately 145 miles of state
highway, excluding I-76, located in Morgan County, have low drivability
life pursuant to a 2023 CDOT assessment; and
WHEREAS, Accordingly, seventy percent of the state highway miles
in Morgan County have a drivability life of three years or less, according
to the 2023 assessment; and
WHEREAS, The last assessment of these state highway miles was
conducted in 2023, and as such, the identified segments of state highway
with low drivability life in Morgan County likely have a year or less of
drivability life in 2025; and
WHEREAS, Morgan County has been significantly underserved by
CDOT through its project and maintenance planning; now, therefore,

Be It Resolved by the Senate of the Seventy-fifth General Assembly of
the State of Colorado:
That the Senate supports the Morgan County Board of County
Commissioners in demanding CDOT to reevaluate its priorities to provide
funding to undertake road improvement projects in Morgan County and
to implement those projects.
Be It Further Resolved, That copies of this Senate Resolution be sent
to Shoshana M. Lew, the executive director of CDOT.
