2 WHEREAS, Upon his return from the service, Senator Entz took
3 up his family's farming tradition, opening a successful 160-acre potato
4 farm in Hooper, Colorado. The operation would grow to over 1,000 acres.
5 He continued working on his farm even while serving in the legislature,
6 telling the Colorado Foundation for Water Education that he was often at
7 his "legislative desk for roll call in the morning and back to the farm the
8 same afternoon"; and
9 WHEREAS, Senator Entz won his first election for public office
10 in 1969, as an Alamosa County Commissioner, and served on the
11 Alamosa Board of County Commissioners until 1982. Senator Entz was
12 the chairperson of the Board for eight years of his tenure; and
13 WHEREAS, In 1983, voters in House District 60 elected Senator
14 Entz to the first of eight consecutive terms in the Colorado House of
15 Representatives. Throughout his tenure in the House of Representatives,
16 in which he represented Alamosa, Conejos, Costilla, Gunnison, Hinsdale,
17 Huerfano, Las Animas, Mineral, Rio Grande, and Saguache counties,
18 Senator Entz played a key role in issues related to agriculture, local
19 government, energy, natural resources, and veterans' and military affairs.
20 Senator Entz chaired the House Local Government committee from 1989
21 through 1994; the Agriculture, Livestock, and Natural Resources
22 committee from 1995 through 1998; and the interim Wildlife legislative
23 committee in 1995; and
24 WHEREAS, After leaving the House of Representatives in 1998,
25 Senator Entz served on the Colorado Water Conservation Board for two
26 years, including as the Board's chair in 2000; and
27 WHEREAS, In 2001, a vacancy committee selected Senator Entz
28 to fill the open District 5 seat in the Colorado State Senate, replacing
29 Senator Gigi Dennis after she left to join the U.S. Department of
30 Agriculture. Upon serving out the remainder of Senator Dennis' term, in
31 which Senator Entz represented Alamosa, Costilla, Conejos, Custer,
32 Mineral, Huerfano, Las Animas, Pueblo, Rio Grande, and Saguache
33 counties, Senator Entz defeated two opponents to win reelection in 2002.
34 Reapportionment in 2002 changed the boundaries of his district to
35 Alamosa, Chaffee, Conejos, Costilla, Delta, Gunnison, Hinsdale, Mineral,
36 Pitkin, Rio Grande, and Saguache counties. In his more than five years in
37 the Senate, Senator Entz continued to be involved with issues related to

1 veterans' affairs, agriculture, natural resources, and local government,
2 among others. He chaired the Senate Agriculture, Natural Resources, and
3 Energy committee in 2003 and 2004 and was a member of the legislative
4 Water Legislation Review and legislative Water Resources Review
5 committees from 2001 through 2005, serving as chair of the legislative
6 Water Resources Review committee in 2003 and as vice chair in 2004;
7 and
8 WHEREAS, As a legislator, Senator Entz carried nearly 70 water
9 bills, including the 2004 bill that created Subdistrict One in the Rio
10 Grande Water Conservation District, first-of-its-kind legislation that
11 became a model for the rest of the country. In 2007, the Colorado Water
12 Congress presented Senator Entz the Wayne Aspinall "Water Leader of
13 the Year" award, and, in 2012, the Colorado Foundation for Water
14 Education awarded him the President's Award for lifetime achievement
15 in water education; and
16 WHEREAS, Senator Entz sponsored House Bill 88-1250, which
17 created the Colorado Division of Aeronautics. The signing of the bill on
18 May 24, 1988, took place on the wing of Senator Entz's aircraft.
19 Additionally, he received the "Lifetime Achievement Award" from the
20 Colorado Aeronautical Board for his crucial role in its establishment; and
21 WHEREAS, Senator Entz's former colleague in the state
22 legislature and then U.S. Representative John Salazar paid tribute to
23 Senator Entz in the U.S. House, saying that Senator Entz's "vast
24 knowledge of Colorado's complex water laws and the importance of
25 water to the San Luis Valley was essential, and he worked tirelessly to
26 protect water," and that "No one served the people of the San Luis Valley
27 more vigorously than Entz"; and
28 WHEREAS, Adams State College awarded Senator Entz an
29 honorary doctorate degree in 2007; and
30 WHEREAS, Following the death of Lora Entz in 2014, Senator
31 Entz married Kathryn "Kitty" Mary Bigley Entz in 2020; and
32 WHEREAS, Senator Entz was active in his community outside of
33 political office. He was a well-known pilot and aviator in the region, and
34 he frequented parades across the San Luis Valley, in which he drove his
35 restored 1943 Ford jeep. Senator Entz participated in the Alamosa
36 Veterans Day Parade every year, and he displayed his passion for

1 restoring cars and tractors through the Early Iron Festival car show in
2 Alamosa; and
3 WHEREAS, Current and former legislators remember Senator
4 Entz as a mentor and friend, including current Senate Minority Leader
5 Cleave Simpson and former state senators Gigi Dennis and Larry
6 Crowder. "Such an honorable public servant; I am so proud to have
7 known him and [worked] with him on important Valley issues," Simpson
8 told Colorado Politics in December. "I work with folks at the Capitol
9 today who still recall his legislative prowess and professionalism"; and
10 WHEREAS, Senator Entz was preceded in death by his first wife,
11 Lora Entz; his parents; nine siblings; and two grandchildren; and
12 WHEREAS, Senator Entz is survived by his wife, Kathryn "Kitty"
13 Mary Bigley Entz; daughters Cynthia Chapman, Cassandra Curtis, and
14 Catherine Wright; son Michael A. Entz; sister Vivian Snow; five
15 grandchildren; and seven great-grandchildren; now, therefore,
16 Be It Resolved by the Senate of the Seventy-fifth General Assembly
17 of the State of Colorado, the House of Representatives concurring herein:
18 That, in the death of Senator Lewis H. Entz, the people of the State
19 of Colorado have lost a devoted public servant and an outstanding citizen,
20 and that we, the members of the Senate of the Seventy-fifth General
21 Assembly, pay tribute to Lewis H. Entz for years of dedicated public
22 service and extend our deep and heartfelt sympathy to the members of the
23 family.
24 Be It Further Resolved, That copies of this Memorial be sent to
25 Kathryn "Kitty" Mary Bigley Entz, Cynthia Chapman, Cassandra Curtis,
