CONCERNING THE DESIGNATION OF "COLORADO 4-H
DAY".
WHEREAS, 4-H is America's largest youth development
organization, having supported almost 6 million youth across the country
thus far; and
WHEREAS, 4-H helps Colorado youth develop the "four Hs": Head,
Heart, Hands, and Health; and
WHEREAS, 4-H annually reaches over 110,000 youth in Colorado,
helping them become confident, independent, resilient, and
compassionate leaders; and
WHEREAS, 4-H is nationally delivered by Cooperative Extension--a
community of more than 100 public universities across the nation,
including the Colorado State University Extension--that provides
experiences for young people to learn by doing hands-on projects in areas
including health, science, agriculture, and citizenship; and

WHEREAS, 4-H's local network of over 10,000 volunteers and almost
100 professionals provides caring and supportive mentoring to Colorado
youth, helping them to grow into true leaders, entrepreneurs, and
visionaries; now, therefore,
Be It Resolved by the Senate of the Seventy-fifth General Assembly of
the State of Colorado, the House of Representatives concurring herein:
That the General Assembly hereby declares Monday, January 26,
2026, to be "Colorado 4-H Day" in recognition of the 4-H program's
extraordinary efforts on behalf of Colorado's youth.
Be It Further Resolved, That copies of this Joint Resolution be sent to
Governor Jared Polis and Colorado's Commissioner of Agriculture, Kate
Greenberg.
