CONCERNING THE CONTINUATION OF THE VETERINARY PHARMACEUTICAL
ADVISORY COMMITTEE, AND, IN CONNECTION THEREWITH,
IMPLEMENTING THE RECOMMENDATION CONTAINED IN THE 2025
SUNSET REPORT BY THE DEPARTMENT OF REGULATORY AGENCIES TO
REPEAL THE VETERINARY PHARMACEUTICAL ADVISORY COMMITTEE.

heard, may fine a registrant who THAT distributes a veterinary drug in
