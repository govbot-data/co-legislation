101 CONCERNING HONORING KOREAN WAR VETERANS.
1 WHEREAS, The Korean War, often called the "Forgotten War",

3 soldiers and allies fought to defend freedom and democracy under harsh
4 conditions; and

6 home to little recognition, yet their courage and commitment remain a
7 testament to their patriotism; and
8 WHEREAS, The sacrifices of these veterans are now more fully
9 appreciated, and their legacy has become an essential part of our nation's
10 history; now, therefore,
