CONCERNING HONORING KOREAN WAR VETERANS.
WHEREAS, The Korean War, often called the "Forgotten War", took
place from 1950 through 1953, during which brave American soldiers and
allies fought to defend freedom and democracy under harsh conditions;
and
WHEREAS, Korean War veterans sacrificed greatly, returning home
to little recognition, yet their courage and commitment remain a testament
to their patriotism; and
WHEREAS, The sacrifices of these veterans are now more fully
appreciated, and their legacy has become an essential part of our nation's
history; now, therefore,
Be It Resolved by the House of Representatives of the Seventy-fifth
General Assembly of the State of Colorado, the Senate concurring herein:

That we honor and recognize the Korean War veterans for their
service, acknowledging that while the war was once "forgotten", their
sacrifice will never be forgotten again.
