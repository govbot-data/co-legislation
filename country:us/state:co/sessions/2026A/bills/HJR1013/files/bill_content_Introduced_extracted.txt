HOUSE JOINT RESOLUTION 26-1013
101 CONCERNING RECOGNITION AND REMEMBRANCE OF COLORADO
102 MILITARY VETERANS WHO SERVED IN THE PERSIAN GULF WAR.
1 WHEREAS, American involvement in the Persian Gulf War began
2 in 1990 with Operation Desert Shield, which marked the deployment of
3 United States service members to Saudi Arabia in response to the Iraqi
4 invasion of Kuwait; and
5 WHEREAS, The deployment of United States service members to
6 the Persian Gulf marked the beginning of a six-month-long war with Iraq
7 that would eventually end following a successful aerial bombing
8 campaign and ground invasion referred to as Operation Desert Storm; and
9 WHEREAS, The United States led a forty-two-country
10 international coalition-the largest since World War II-with the purpose of

1 liberating Iraqi-occupied Kuwait; and
2 WHEREAS, One hundred forty-three members of the United
3 States Armed Forces, including three Coloradans, were killed in action
4 over the course of the Persian Gulf War; and
5 WHEREAS, It is, and will forever remain, society's solemn
6 obligation to recognize the service rendered by Persian Gulf War veterans
7 and the sacrifice associated with that service; now, therefore,
8 Be It Resolved by the House of Representatives of the Seventy-fifth
9 General Assembly of the State of Colorado, the Senate concurring herein:
10 That we, the members of the Colorado General Assembly, hereby:
11 (1) Acknowledge and recognize the veterans of the Persian Gulf
12 War for the service they rendered;
13 (2) Honor those who made the ultimate sacrifice and gave their
14 lives in service to their country;
15 (3) Express our heartfelt gratitude and sympathy to the families of
16 fallen service members; and
17 (4) Encourage all Coloradans to remember the sacrifices made by
18 Gulf War veterans and their families.
19 Be It Further Resolved, That copies of this Joint Resolution be sent
20 to the Colorado Department of Military and Veterans Affairs; the United
21 Veterans Coalition of Colorado; President of the United States, Donald
22 J. Trump; Governor of Colorado, the honorable Jared Polis; and the
23 Colorado Congressional delegation.
