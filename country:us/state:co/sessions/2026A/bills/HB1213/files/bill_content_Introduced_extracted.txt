Resources Committee. The bill implements the recommendation of the
department of regulatory agencies' 2025 sunset review and report on the
biomass utilization grant program by repealing the biomass utilization
grant program.
1 Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
2 SECTION 1. In Colorado Revised Statutes, repeal 23-31-317 as
3 follows:
4 23-31-317. Biomass utilization study - legislative declaration
5 - report - definitions - repeal.
6 (1) The general assembly:
7 (a) Finds and determines that:
8 (I) Three of the largest wildfires in Colorado's history occurred in
9 2020, with more than six hundred twenty-five thousand acres burned
10 across the state;
11 (II) It is estimated that two hundred fourteen million dollars was
12 spent in Colorado in 2020 to fight forest fires;
13 (III) With almost three million people in Colorado residing in the
14 wildland-urban interface, these wildfires threaten human life as well as
15 private property, public infrastructure, and the environment;
16 (IV) The forest service has implemented a number of strategies to
17 help mitigate the risk of wildfire, including engaging in educational
18 outreach, providing technical assistance to communities in the WUI with
19 the development of community wildfire protection plans, and treating
20 forested lands to reduce the amount of fuel;
21 (V) One promising strategy for wildfire mitigation is to increase
22 the utilization of biomass to reduce fuel; and
23 (VI) Increased biomass utilization would provide other

1 environmental benefits such as:
2 (A) Using biomass for electric and heat generation as a means to
3 further diversify Colorado's renewable energy portfolio and, in
4 furtherance of the governor's "Greenhouse Gas Pollution Reduction
5 Roadmap" released on January 14, 2021, provide a carbon-neutral
6 alternative energy source to fossil fuels; and
7 (B) Applying biochar to soil as a means to improve soil health and
8 provide carbon sequestration; and
9 (b) Declares that it is in the interest of the state for the forest
10 service to administer a grant program to demonstrate biomass utilization
11 as a means to innovate wildfire mitigation, renewable energy
12 development, soil health, climate change mitigation, and carbon
13 sequestration.
14 (2) As used in this section, unless the context otherwise requires:
15 (a) "Biochar" means a charcoal that is produced by pyrolysis of
16 biomass and is used as a soil amendment.
17 (b) "Biomass" has the meaning set forth in section 40-2-124
18 (1)(a)(I).
19 (c) "Forest service" has the meaning set forth in section 23-31-310
20 (2)(c).

22 (e) "Pyrolysis" has the meaning set forth in section 40-2-124
23 (1)(a)(V).
24 (f) "Wildland-urban interface" or "WUI" has the meaning set forth
25 in section 23-31-310 (2)(f).
