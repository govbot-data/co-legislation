Bill Summary
(Note: This summary applies to this bill as introduced and does
not reflect any amendments that may be subsequently adopted. If this bill
passes third reading in the house of introduction, a bill summary that
applies to the reengrossed version of this bill will be available at
http://leg.colorado.gov.)
The bill prohibits a member or member-elect of the general
assembly from holding any other elected offices while serving as a
member of the general assembly or while a member-elect of the general
assembly.

1 Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
2 SECTION 1. In Colorado Revised Statutes, add 2-2-328 as
3 follows:
4 2-2-328. Members of the general assembly prohibited from
5 holding multiple elected offices.
6 (1) COMMENCING WITH THE FIRST REGULAR SESSION OF THE
7 SEVENTY-SIXTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY, AND DURING EACH REGULAR
8 SESSION THEREAFTER, EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SUBSECTION (2) OF THIS
9 SECTION, A MEMBER OF THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY SHALL NOT HOLD ANY
10 OTHER ELECTED OFFICE WHILE SERVING AS A MEMBER OF THE GENERAL
11 ASSEMBLY. THIS SECTION APPLIES TO A MEMBER OF THE GENERAL
12 ASSEMBLY UPON TAKING THE OATH OF OFFICE OF THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY
13 AS REQUIRED BY SECTION 2 (2) OF ARTICLE V OF THE STATE
14 CONSTITUTION.
15 (2) THE PROVISIONS OF SUBSECTION (1) OF THIS SECTION DO NOT
16 APPLY IF:
17 (a) THE MEMBER OF THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY HOLDS A SPECIAL
18 DISTRICT ELECTED OFFICE;
19 (b) THE MEMBER OF THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY HAS LESS THAN ONE
20 YEAR REMAINING IN THEIR TERM IN ANOTHER ELECTED OFFICE AT THE
21 TIME THE MEMBER TAKES THE OATH OF OFFICE OF THE GENERAL
22 ASSEMBLY AS REQUIRED BY SECTION 2 (2) OF ARTICLE V OF THE STATE
23 CONSTITUTION;
24 (c) THE MEMBER OF THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY HAS LESS THAN ONE
25 YEAR REMAINING IN THEIR TERM IN THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY AT THE TIME
26 THE MEMBER TAKES THE OATH OF OFFICE OF ANOTHER ELECTED OFFICE;
27 OR
