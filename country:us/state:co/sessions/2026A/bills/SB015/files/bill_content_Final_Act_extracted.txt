CONCERNING OFFENSES INVOLVING COMMERCIAL SEXUAL ACTIVITY WITH A
CHILD.
Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
SECTION 1. Legislative declaration. (1) The general assembly
finds and declares that:
(a) Because of the harms suffered by victims of crimes involving
commercial sexual activity with a child, the general assembly encourages
courts to exercise their discretion to sentence persons convicted of soliciting
for commercial sexual activity with a child to the department of corrections
for a term of at least the minimum of the presumptive range for a class 3

(b) In making a determination regarding sentencing, diversion,

is a current victim of human trafficking or has experienced coercion, force,

SECTION 2. In Colorado Revised Statutes, repeal and reenact,
with amendments, 18-7-401 as follows:
18-7-401. Definitions.
AS USED IN THIS PART 4, UNLESS THE CONTEXT OTHERWISE REQUIRES:
(1) "CHILD" MEANS A PERSON WHO IS UNDER EIGHTEEN YEARS OF
AGE.

18-6-403 (3)(a) AND (3)(d); OR
(e) AN OBSCENE PERFORMANCE, AS DEFINED IN SECTION 18-7-101.
