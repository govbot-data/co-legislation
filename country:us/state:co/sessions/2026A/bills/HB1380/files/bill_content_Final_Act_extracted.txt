HOUSE BILL 26-1380
BY REPRESENTATIVE(S) Brown and Taggart, Sirota, Duran, Espenoza;
also SENATOR(S) Bridges and Kirkmeyer, Amabile.
CONCERNING THE REPEAL OF THE OFFICE OF THE JUDICIAL DISCIPLINE
OMBUDSMAN.
Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Colorado:
SECTION 1. In Colorado Revised Statutes, 13-3-120, add (18) as
follows:
13-3-120. Office of the judicial discipline ombudsman
established - judicial discipline advisory board - qualifications of
ombudsman - powers and duties - confidentiality - rules - definitions -
repeal.
(18) THIS SECTION IS REPEALED, EFFECTIVE JULY 1, 2027.
SECTION 2. Safety clause. The general assembly finds,
determines, and declares that this act is necessary for the immediate
preservation of the public peace, health, or safety or for appropriations for
